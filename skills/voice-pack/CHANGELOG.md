**Changes since 3.0.0-beta.4** (what 3.0.0 changes from 2.x is in the 3.0.0-beta.1 entry of `CHANGELOG.md`) / **和 3.0.0-beta.4 比，改了什么**（3.0.0 和 2.x 比改了什么，见 `CHANGELOG.md` 里 3.0.0-beta.1 那一条）：

- **Who the lines are to** (`references/character-voice.md` 「台本对谁说：见到创作者时说的话」). The audition text, the default segment's passage, the card line and a passage for an added language are what the character says on seeing its creator, like a gacha home-screen character to the player. Who the creator is and what the character calls them come from the card and the other material; when nothing says, someone it knows, called "you", never an invented title. The card's scene is background. No self-introduction. The default segment runs a few such lines together in three or four moods. Before, the audition text was a self-introduction and the card line was said to whoever opens the card. / **台本对谁说**（`references/character-voice.md`「台本对谁说：见到创作者时说的话」）：试听文本、默认段那段、卡片那一句、补另一种语言的那段，都写成角色见到创作者时会说的话，像二游主界面的角色对玩家说话。创作者是谁、怎么称呼，从角色卡和别的材料推断；没说的，当熟人，称「你」，不编头衔。卡上的情境是背景。不自我介绍。默认段是这样的几句连着说，三四种心情。以前试听文本是自我介绍，卡片那一句是对打开卡片的人说的。
- **Designed voices: describe what can be heard** (`models/qwen-audio-3.1-tts-flash.md`, `SKILL.md` section 2). A few sentences after the vendor's current guide, whose table gives examples, not the only words; accent, rasp and breath go in too, though often not produced; an accent only when the card or user names one. The audition text keeps the character's catchphrases. Readings of 2026-11-07 are in the model note. / **设计出来的声音：描述写听得出来的特点**（`models/qwen-audio-3.1-tts-flash.md`，`SKILL.md` 第 2 节）：照厂商现在的指南写几句，表里的词是示例；口音、沙哑、气声也写进去，常常做不出来；口音只在角色卡或使用者说了时写。试听文本保留口头禅。2026-11-07 的读数在模型说明里。
- **Punctuation for what should be heard** (`models/scene3.md` 「标点」, `scenes/one-line.md`, `scenes/a-scene.md`). Readings of 2026-11-07, 549 one-line requests, measured and judged by a model, three pairs heard by one person: `……` between words is a hesitation; a stammer repeats the word with `，` or `……` (a listener could not tell `——` from a comma); no commas sounds rushed; a final `～` draws the end out; no mark makes a line break off. After a `……`, check the rest was spoken (1 in 72 dropped it). / **标点照想听到的效果下**（`models/scene3.md`「标点」，`scenes/one-line.md`，`scenes/a-scene.md`）：2026-11-07 的读数，549 条一句一个请求，程序量加模型判，三对一个人听过。词和词之间的 `……` 是犹豫；结巴重复那个字，中间写 `，` 或 `……`（人听不出 `——` 和逗号的分别）；去掉逗号听着急；句尾 `～` 拖长；没有标点做得出被打断。用了 `……` 的，配完查后面的话念了没有（72 条丢过 1 条）。
- **The card line** (`references/character-voice.md` 「卡片试听」) has no phoneme tags (a reading goes in the lexicon, so the card keeps the word) and no stacked marks or `?!`. / **卡片那一句**（`references/character-voice.md`「卡片试听」）不写音素标签（读音写进读音词表，卡上留原词），不用连写的标点和 `?!`。
- **Model notes** (`models/scene3.md` 「参考」「写什么」): on 2026-11-07, after scene 3 spoke a passage from each designed audition, different characters came out more alike to a speaker model (0.03 to 0.07 on average); throat and accent words in every cue did not pull them apart. / **模型说明**（`models/scene3.md`「参考」「写什么」）：2026-11-07，scene 3 照设计出来的试听配一段话以后，不同角色在说话人模型上更像了（平均高 0.03 到 0.07）；每处提示写嗓子、口音的词也没把它们分开。

## 3.0.0-beta.3 — 2026-11

**Pre-release** of 3.0.0, like 3.0.0-beta.1 (the full note is in that entry): in the version index and signed, while `npx skills add` and the plugin marketplace still install 2.7.0. Packs made with it ask for `3.0.0-beta.3` or later.
**预发布版**，同 3.0.0-beta.1（完整的说明见那一条）：进版本索引、签名，`npx skills add` 和插件市场装的仍是 2.7.0。用它做的包要求 `3.0.0-beta.3` 或更新。

**Changes since 3.0.0-beta.2** (what 3.0.0 changes from 2.x is in the 3.0.0-beta.1 entry of `CHANGELOG.md`) / **和 3.0.0-beta.2 比，改了什么**（3.0.0 和 2.x 比改了什么，见 `CHANGELOG.md` 里 3.0.0-beta.1 那一条）：

- **Adding a language: the pack voices the reference itself first** (`dub_cross_language`, `card_line_cross_language`, `references/character-voice.md` 「补一段这种语言的参考」). When the user wants no accent and a reference in the line's language has to be added, and there is no recording of the character speaking that language (a designed voice too, and a pack already made that needs another language), the default is now to have the pack voice a passage in that language across languages (`dub --language <language> --allow-cross-language`): two or three takes, each further one with `--only 1` and a new seed; the one the user picks by ear (or the first, if they do not want to pick) goes to `make-pack --previous` as a segment marked `"synthetic": true`. It is not picked by speaker similarity: the speaker model also hears language and accent, so the take most like the original-language reference may be the most accented. Before voicing it, the agent says it may carry a little of the original language's accent; it does not first ask whether an accent is wanted. A recording of the character in that language is still used when there is one, and another voice the user picks from the library is still an option. Wherever the agent lists where the reference can come from (the question after `dub_cross_language` or `card_line_cross_language`, and its reply once the reference is added), and when the user later says that language has an accent, it also lists voicing the passage with another model first and cloning from that, worded 「先用 <模型名> 念一遍再克隆：想压一压口音可以试」 with the model's name as the platform gives it and nothing added about how well it works. Taking it, the other model's passage and the original-language default segment are given together as that language's reference, the original first: joined into one segment (edges trimmed, peaks matched, 0.3 s of silence between them, at most 19.5 s, as the model joins several references itself), the two texts joined with a space in the same order, `annotatedText` given too, and the card line voiced in that language with `--card-line`. The base part never switches models itself, so this is listed only where the platform can name the model (how, in the platform's adapter). The messages of `dub_cross_language` and `card_line_cross_language` and the text output of `make-pack` now say where the reference comes from (`referenceSources` in `lib/cross-language.mjs`); the error codes and `details` are unchanged. Before, the only sources were a recording of the character and a voice from the library, and for a designed voice already made the step was left open. / **给包加一种语言：先用这个包自己配出这种语言的参考**（`dub_cross_language`、`card_line_cross_language`，`references/character-voice.md`「补一段这种语言的参考」）：使用者不要口音、要给包补一段这种语言的参考，又没有这个角色说这种语言的录音的（设计出来的声音也一样，已经做好的包要补另一种语言也一样），默认用这个包跨语言配一段这种语言的话当参考（`dub --language <这种语言> --allow-cross-language`）：配两三条，后面的每条用 `--only 1` 再配、换一个 seed；请使用者听了挑（他不想挑就用第一条），不按说话人相似度挑：说话人模型也会抓语言和口音，和原来那种语言的参考最像的那条，可能口音最重；挑中的当素材交给 `make-pack --previous`，标 `"synthetic": true`。配之前说一句可能带一点原来那种语言的口音，不先问要不要口音。有这个角色说这种语言的录音的照旧用录音，使用者在库里挑的另一个声音照旧能用。列参考从哪来的选项时（`dub_cross_language`、`card_line_cross_language` 停下来以后的那一问，补完参考以后的回复），和使用者之后专门说这种语言有口音时，一直多列一项「先用 <模型名> 念一遍再克隆：想压一压口音可以试」，模型名照平台的写，不另加效果怎样的话。走这一项时，另一个模型念的那段和原来那种语言的默认段一起当这种语言的参考，原来的在前：接成一段（两头去掉静音、峰值拉齐、中间 0.3 秒静音，不超过 19.5 秒，模型那边收到几段参考也是这样接的），两段的文字照同样的次序用空格接起来，`annotatedText` 一起交，这种语言的卡片那一句用 `--card-line` 一起配。基础部分自己不换模型，所以平台能指定模型时才列这一项，怎么调看平台的适配器。`dub_cross_language`、`card_line_cross_language` 的说明和 `make-pack` 的文字输出跟着说参考从哪来（`lib/cross-language.mjs` 的 `referenceSources`），错误码和 `details` 不变。原来来源只有这个角色的录音和库里的声音，设计出来的声音做好以后怎么补没定。
- **Model notes** (`models/scene3.md`, `models/qwen-audio-3.1-tts-flash.md`). 「跨语言和口音」 adds a reading of two references given together across languages (2026-11-06): the Japanese original and an English passage voiced from it by another model, used together to voice the same English line, 10 seeds in each order, 20 takes, measured with a speaker model. Against the Japanese reference they averaged 0.60 and 0.62, about the same as voicing directly (0.61) and 0.08 to 0.10 above going through the other model first (0.53); against the English passage 0.70 and 0.75, about the same as going through the other model (0.72) and 0.15 to 0.20 above voicing directly (0.55). Seeds moved them about half as much as voicing directly (not significant), and the best direct take (0.783) is above every two-reference take. No one has listened to them yet. The section also says how a reference in another language is added by default and that voicing it with another model first is kept as an option, and where that requirement came from; the other model's notes say that is all it is used for there. / **模型说明**（`models/scene3.md`、`models/qwen-audio-3.1-tts-flash.md`）：「跨语言和口音」补了两段参考一起给、跨语言配的读数（2026-11-06）：日语原声，和另一个模型照它念的一段英文，一起当参考配同一句英文，两种次序各 10 个 seed，共 20 条，用声纹模型量。和日语参考比：两种次序平均 0.60、0.62，和直接配（0.61）差不多，比先用另一个模型的（0.53）高 0.08 到 0.10；和那段英文比：0.70、0.75，和先用另一个模型的（0.72）差不多，比直接配（0.55）高 0.15 到 0.20。seed 之间的波动约是直接配的一半（不显著），直接配最好的一条（0.783）比两段的哪一条都高。还没有人听过。这一节还写了给包补另一种语言的参考默认怎么补、先用另一个模型念一遍留作选项，和这条要求的出处；另一个模型的说明写明它在这里只是这个选项。

## 3.0.0-beta.2 — 2026-11

**Pre-release** of 3.0.0, like 3.0.0-beta.1 (the full note is in that entry): in the version index and signed, while `npx skills add` and the plugin marketplace still install 2.7.0. Packs made with it ask for `3.0.0-beta.2` or later.
**预发布版**，同 3.0.0-beta.1（完整的说明见那一条）：进版本索引、签名，`npx skills add` 和插件市场装的仍是 2.7.0。用它做的包要求 `3.0.0-beta.2` 或更新。

**Changes since 3.0.0-beta.1** (what 3.0.0 changes from 2.x is in the 3.0.0-beta.1 entry of `CHANGELOG.md`) / **和 3.0.0-beta.1 比，改了什么**（3.0.0 和 2.x 比改了什么，见 `CHANGELOG.md` 里 3.0.0-beta.1 那一条）：

- **No accent by default** (`--allow-cross-language`, `dub_cross_language`). A reference in one language reading another usually carries its accent, and cues do not remove it (`models/scene3.md` 「跨语言和口音」). So when a line to be voiced in this run would use a reference segment that is not in the line's language (a pack with only Japanese references, an English script), `dub` stops before anything is paid, dry runs included, with `dub_cross_language`: which units (numbered as for `--only`), which pack and segment, and the reference's language → the line's language (`ja→en`; where the text alone cannot tell which language, its script, `ja→拉丁字母`). `details.units` is `[{ unit, pack: { id, name }, speaker?, segmentId, by, from, to, script?, lines, hasLanguage }]`; in the library, `planDub` and `dubScript` throw it. The message gives two ways and leaves the choice to the user: keep the accent with `--allow-cross-language` (`allowCrossLanguage: true` in the library), which dubs across languages as before and is kept in the redo commands `dub` prints; or add a reference in that language and make a new version with `make-pack --previous`. Where the pack has that language after all (`hasLanguage`), it also says how to reach it (another `--reference-tag`, leaving out `--language` or giving one over the setup's, another `--setup`, or `--no-scene`). In a scene sent as one request, each speaker's one reference is checked against every one of his turns. Not stopped: a few words of another language inside a line (still marked `pick.crossLanguage`), a line whose language cannot be told, and lines that `--only` does not name or that are carried over. An `allowCrossLanguage` that is not true or false is `dub_input_invalid` (`make_pack_input_invalid` in `makePack`), with `field` `allowCrossLanguage`. A caller whose whole lines used a reference in another language (lines in a language the pack has no reference for, a reference tag the line's language lacks, a `--language` or setup language the text plainly is not, or a scene where one speaker's turns change language) adds `--allow-cross-language` to keep the old behaviour. / **默认不带口音**（`--allow-cross-language`、`dub_cross_language`）：用一种语言的参考念另一种语言，常带参考那种语言的口音，写提示也去不掉（`models/scene3.md`「跨语言和口音」）。所以这一次要合成的句子里有一句用的参考段不是这一句的语言（包里只有日语的参考，台本是英文），`dub` 在花钱之前停下，试运行也停，报 `dub_cross_language`：哪几段（编号同 `--only`）、哪个包、哪段参考、参考段的语言→台词的语言（`ja→en`；只从文字认不出是哪一种语言的，写文字，`ja→拉丁字母`）。`details.units` 是 `[{ unit, pack: { id, name }, speaker?, segmentId, by, from, to, script?, lines, hasLanguage }]`；库里是 `planDub`、`dubScript` 抛它。说明给两条路，由使用者定：要口音，加 `--allow-cross-language`（库里是 `allowCrossLanguage: true`），照以前那样跨语言克隆配，`dub` 打印的重做命令也带着它；不要口音，给包补一段这种语言的参考，用 `make-pack --previous` 发新版本。包里其实有这种语言的（`hasLanguage`），说明另说怎么用上它（换一个 `--reference-tag`，不给 `--language` 或者给 `--language` 盖过设定的，换一套 `--setup`，`--no-scene`）。一场戏一个请求的，每个说话人整场用的那段参考和他的每一轮都比。不停的：一行里夹着另一种语言的几个字（照旧标 `pick.crossLanguage`）、看不出语言的一行、`--only` 没点到的和沿用的句子。`allowCrossLanguage` 不是 true、false 的，报 `dub_input_invalid`（`makePack` 里是 `make_pack_input_invalid`），`field` 是 `allowCrossLanguage`。以前整行用另一种语言的参考配的调用方（给包里没有的语言配音，标签在这一行的语言里没有，给的 `--language` 或设定写的语言文字看得出不是，或者一场戏里一个说话人的几轮换了语言），要照旧那样配，加 `--allow-cross-language`。
- **A line's language is its main script.** A katakana name in a Chinese line, or ありがとう in an English line, no longer makes the line Japanese: a kana weighs as much as a Han character, and Han characters count as Japanese only when kana are at least a fifth of kana and Han together. `make-pack` reads a segment's language from its text the same way, and so does `languageTagOf`. A language given with `--language` or by a setup that the text plainly is not (English lines given `ja`) is checked against the text's language, so it stops too; where the text cannot tell (only digits and punctuation, or languages that share a script, such as English and French), the given language stands. The `language_cut` note now says to keep a short mixed passage in one line, or that `--language` makes the other language's lines cross-language, which needs `--allow-cross-language`. / **一行的语言看主要的文字**：一行中文里一个片假名的名字、一行英文里一句ありがとう，不再让这一行算日文：一个假名和一个汉字一样算，假名至少占汉字加假名的五分之一，汉字才算日文的。`make-pack` 从文字认段的语言、`languageTagOf` 也照这一条。`--language` 或设定给的语言，文字看得出不是它的（英文台词给了 `ja`），按文字认的比，照样停；文字看不出的（只有数字、标点，或者同一种文字的几种语言，像英语和法语），照给的比。说明 `language_cut` 现在说：一小段混着语言的话写在同一行里；或者给 `--language`，另一种语言的整行就是跨语言克隆，要加 `--allow-cross-language`。
- **The card line follows the same rule** (`card_line_cross_language`). With `make-pack --card-line`, a card line whose chosen reference is in another language is not voiced and nothing is called for it; the pack is still made, with the default segment on the card (`report.cardLine.status` `failed`, `error.code` `dub_cross_language`, no attempts). The material hints give `card_line_cross_language` (`details: { segmentId, from, to, script?, hasLanguage }`) instead of `card_line_failed`, and `make-pack --allow-cross-language` voices it. The fidelity trials still clone across languages, and `make-pack --dry-run` does not check the card line's language. / **卡片那一句照同一条**（`card_line_cross_language`）：`make-pack --card-line` 给卡片那一句挑的参考段不是它的语言的，不配，一次都不调，包照做，卡片上放默认段（`report.cardLine.status` 是 `failed`，`error.code` 是 `dub_cross_language`，没有尝试）。「还缺什么素材」里是 `card_line_cross_language`（`details: { segmentId, from, to, script?, hasLanguage }`），不是 `card_line_failed`；`make-pack --allow-cross-language` 照配。量还原度的试合成照旧跨语言也配；`make-pack --dry-run` 不查这一句的语言。
- **About 1000 spoken characters a request by default** (`maxSpokenChars`, `cut_to_fit`). Without `--max-chars`, `dub` keeps a request to about 1000 spoken characters (`DEFAULT_MAX_SPOKEN_CHARS` in `lib/synthesis-limits.mjs`, about 170 English words), narration and scenes alike. Counted by `spokenLength`: cues and tag names are not counted, words wrapped in tags are, a phoneme tag counts as one, and a run of whitespace counts as one. It cuts at scene, line and speaker boundaries first, inside a line between sentences, then at a comma or a similar mark; this limit never cuts between characters, so a long sentence with no such mark stays one request. An adapter's `limits.maxChars` (characters sent, opening cue included) must hold as well, and only it cuts between characters; a piece left with only punctuation joins the one before. `--max-chars` replaces both and counts characters sent; in the library, `maxSpokenChars` sets this limit (an integer of at least 10; anything else but `null` is `dub_input_invalid`) and `null` turns it off. Before, nothing was cut by length unless a limit was given or declared. The `details` of `cut_to_fit` gain `maxSpokenChars` (null when that limit is off), and `maxChars` is null when there is none. Running the same command again over an `--out` dubbed with 3.0.0-beta.1 may cut a long text differently: changed pieces are voiced and billed again, and the `--only` numbers change. Library: `requestsOf` takes `limits` (a list of `{ max, measure, extra? }`) instead of `max`, `measure` and `extra`. / **默认一个请求约 1000 个要念的字符**（`maxSpokenChars`、`cut_to_fit`）：不给 `--max-chars`，`dub` 一个请求不超过约 1000 个要念的字符（`lib/synthesis-limits.mjs` 的 `DEFAULT_MAX_SPOKEN_CHARS`，英文约 170 个词），旁白和多人戏都一样。按 `spokenLength` 数：提示、标签名不算，标签包住的字照算，一个音素标签算一个字，连着的空白算一个。先在场景、行与行、换人的边界切，一行自己放不下才在句子之间切，再在逗号一类的地方；这个上限不在字与字之间切，没有逗号一类的一长句照旧一个请求。适配器的 `limits.maxChars`（发出去的字，连开头的提示）也要满足，只有它在字与字之间硬切；切剩下只有标点的一截并进前一截。给了 `--max-chars` 就只照它切，数的是发出去的字；库里用 `maxSpokenChars` 给这个上限（不小于 10 的整数；不是这样也不是 `null` 的报 `dub_input_invalid`），`null` 是不要它。以前不给、不声明上限就不按长度切。`cut_to_fit` 的 `details` 多了 `maxSpokenChars`（不按它切时是 null），没有上限时 `maxChars` 是 null。用 3.0.0-beta.1 配过的长文，在同一个 `--out` 里再跑同一条命令，切法可能不一样：变了的块重新合成、再计一次费，`--only` 的序号也跟着变。库：`requestsOf` 收 `limits`（`{ max, measure, extra? }` 的列表），不再收 `max`、`measure`、`extra`。
- **A long scene is cut into several** (`cut_to_fit`). Where the environment declares `scene-in-request`, a scene over the limit is now cut between lines into several scenes, each still one request; before, a merged scene was never cut by length. Each speaker keeps one reference across the parts, picked over the whole scene. Where the same speaker goes on across the cut and no pause is written, the later part gets the 540 ms breath (`CUT_PAUSE_MS`, `cutPause` `paragraph`); none is added at a change of speaker. A line too long on its own is voiced alone and cut between sentences, and a part left with one speaker is voiced with `voice-cloning`. The text output says 「放不下一个请求，在…和前一场切开」, and `cut_to_fit` lists these cuts too. Library: `sceneRuns` takes `fits` and returns `{ runs, cuts }`, each run with `whole`. / **长的一场切成几场**（`cut_to_fit`）：环境声明了 `scene-in-request` 的，长过上限的一场现在在行与行之间切成几场，每场照旧一个请求；以前合并的一场从不按长度切。每个说话人在这几场里用同一段参考，按切开以前的整场挑。切口两边是同一个人接着说、台本没写停顿的，后一场前面停 540 毫秒（`CUT_PAUSE_MS`，`cutPause` 是 `paragraph`）；换人处不加。一行自己就放不下的单独配，在句子之间切；切完只剩一个说话人的一场用 `voice-cloning` 配。文字输出写「放不下一个请求，在…和前一场切开」，`cut_to_fit` 也列这些切口。库：`sceneRuns` 收 `fits`，返回 `{ runs, cuts }`，每一场带 `whole`。
- **Every control the dubbing model's vendor documents** (`models/scene3.md` 「提示」). The model notes list the six writings the vendor documents and when to use each: a direction in square brackets; a one-off sound or pause written where it happens (`[shaky breath]`, `[small laugh]`); paired tags that change only the words they wrap (`<whisper>`, `<emphasis>`, `<soft>`, `<fast>`, `<slow>`, `<stress>`, `<forceful>`), inside each turn of a scene too; language tags for a few words in another language (`<french>…</french>`, experimental); phoneme tags (`<|phoneme_start|>…<|phoneme_end|>`); and speaker tags. The readings follow; not tested is no reason to leave a writing out. A direction can be in any language and can carry pace (`[fast, nervous, stumbling over words]`); a bracketed `[slow]` or `[emphasis]` is a direction and reaches on from where it stands, while a paired tag changes only what it wraps, a whole whispered sentence included; an exact speed is still the setup's speed, applied after synthesis. The scene guides and `references/markup.md` (new section 「尖括号标签」) follow these notes. When writing the card line, the agent now leaves out only cues that make the whole sentence quieter (`[quietly]`, `小声` and the like) and a `<whisper>` around the whole sentence, unless the card is a whisper; a tired or sleepy card gets its cue. / **配音模型的厂商文档写了的控制写法都用上**（`models/scene3.md`「提示」）：模型说明列出厂商文档写的六种写法，各写用在什么时候：方括号的说戏；写在发生位置的一下子的声音和停顿（`[shaky breath]`、`[small laugh]`）；只管包住的字的成对标签（`<whisper>`、`<emphasis>`、`<soft>`、`<fast>`、`<slow>`、`<stress>`、`<forceful>`），一场戏的每一轮里也能用；几个另一种语言的字用的语言标签（`<french>…</french>`，实验性）；音素标签（`<|phoneme_start|>…<|phoneme_end|>`）；说话人标签。读数跟在后面；没测过不是不写的理由。说戏用什么语言写都行，也可以带快慢（`[紧张，急促]`）；方括号写的 `[slow]`、`[emphasis]` 是说戏，从那里起往后管，成对标签只管包住的字，一整句耳语也可以整句包住；准的倍数照旧用设定的语速，合成以后变速。场景子 skill 和 `references/markup.md`（新的一节「尖括号标签」）都照这些写。写卡片那一句时，现在只不写整句往小、往轻里压的说戏（`[quietly]`、`小声` 这类）和包住整句的 `<whisper>`，卡上的情境就是悄悄话的除外；卡上的心情是累、困的，照样写说戏。
- **Angle-bracket tags reach the model as written** (`tag_pair_split`). Angle-bracket tags in a script are text, not script markup and not cues, and `dub` passes them on where they are written. The pronunciation lexicon no longer rewrites a tag name or the letters of a phoneme tag (wrapped words are still replaced). A cut for length never falls inside a tag pair or a phoneme tag, and a pair longer than one request is cut inside, with the pair written again around each part. A line's language leaves out tag names, phoneme letters and the words wrapped in a language tag (the 35 language names listed in `tools/markup.mjs`). Sentence splitting, joins and pause writings look through tags; in the library, `wordsOf` drops tags and reads a phoneme tag, or a language-tag pair with its words, as one word, and `firstWrittenChar`, `lastWrittenChar` look through tags. A request whose paired tags do not match, most likely a pair split across two requests by a pause, a line break, a chapter or a change of voice, or a script missing one end, gets a `tag_pair_split` note (`details.units` is `[{ unit, tags }]`); nothing is changed. One exception: in an environment that declares only `phoneme-in-text`, a line that uses a lexicon pinyin phoneme is sent as escaped SSML, and its tags do not reach the model. / **尖括号标签原样到模型**（`tag_pair_split`）：台本里的尖括号标签是文字，不是台本标记，也不是提示，`dub` 原样、按位置交给模型。读音词表不再改标签名和音素标签里的字母（包住的字照样换）。因为放不下切开时，不落在一对标签或一个音素标签里面，一对标签比一个请求还长的，在里面切开，每一段两头补上这一对标签。认一行的语言时不算标签名、音素的字母和语言标签包住的字（语言名是 `tools/markup.mjs` 名单里的 35 个）。分句、两段接起来、写停顿时隔着标签看；库里 `wordsOf` 去掉标签，一个音素标签、一对语言标签连同包住的字各当一个词，`firstWrittenChar`、`lastWrittenChar` 隔着标签看。一个请求里的成对标签没配上对，多半是一对标签被停顿、换行、章节、换声音拆到了两个请求里，也可能是台本少写了一头，规划的说明里有一条 `tag_pair_split`（`details.units` 是 `[{ unit, tags }]`）；只提醒，不改字。例外：只声明了 `phoneme-in-text` 的环境里，用上读音词表拼音音素的一行整行转成转义的 SSML，这一行的标签到不了模型。
- **Lexicon phonemes become phoneme tags** (new declared capability `phoneme-markers`, protocol core 3.0.0-beta.2). In an environment that declares `phoneme-markers`, a `<phoneme>` entry of the pack's `lexicon.pls` replaces the word with `<|phoneme_start|>…<|phoneme_end|>`: pinyin (`x-pinyin`) one tag per syllable, English CMU Arpabet (`x-arpabet`, new) one tag per word, Japanese OpenJTalk-style romaji with pitch digits (`x-romaji`, new) one tag per entry. The tags are written at plan time in the same pass as `<alias>` (longest entry first, replaced text not looked up again), are not escaped, show in the `spoken` of `dub --dry-run`, and are written in every turn of a scene sent as one request; the entries used are listed in `lexicon.phonemes`, and the plan's `capabilities` has `'phoneme-markers': true`. Their characters are sent and billed like any others and count toward `limits.maxChars`: a line of 10 characters with three pinyin tags went out as 116. With both `phoneme-markers` and `phoneme-in-text` declared, the tags win; with only `phoneme-in-text`, pinyin is still sent as SSML, and `x-arpabet` and `x-romaji` stay in `lexicon.unapplied` (`reason: phoneme`). Entries written wrong are `unapplied` with `reason: phoneme_format` (`problem` is `term`, `count` or `form`), and the text output of `dub` says what is wrong for each alphabet. In the library, `lexiconRules(lexicon, { phonemeMarkers })`; the declaration is listed in `vocab/capabilities.json` and `vocab/capability-signatures.md`, and `adapter new` and `adapter check` know it. The tags settle names, rare words and loanwords; where the reading fights a common word in context, the readings found the model saying the common word (`models/scene3.md` 「音素标签」). `references/make-pack-details.md` 「读音词表」 says how to turn a homophone `<alias>` into a `<phoneme>` and what the tags cost. / **读音词表的音素写成音素标签**（新的声明 `phoneme-markers`，协议核心 3.0.0-beta.2）：环境声明了 `phoneme-markers`，包里 `lexicon.pls` 的 `<phoneme>` 词条把词换成 `<|phoneme_start|>…<|phoneme_end|>`：拼音（`x-pinyin`）一个音节一个标签，英文 CMU Arpabet（`x-arpabet`，新加）一个词一个标签，日文带音调数字的 OpenJTalk 风格罗马字（`x-romaji`，新加）一个词条一个标签。标签在规划时和 `<alias>` 在同一次替换里写（长的词条优先，换出来的字不再查词表），不转义，`dub --dry-run` 的 `spoken` 里就看得到，一场戏一个请求时每一轮也写；用上的列在 `lexicon.phonemes`，规划的 `capabilities` 里有 `'phoneme-markers': true`。标签的字和别的字一样发出去、一样算钱，也算进 `limits.maxChars`：10 个字的一句带三个拼音标签，发出去是 116 个字。两个声明都有，用音素标签；只声明了 `phoneme-in-text` 的，拼音照旧写成 SSML，`x-arpabet`、`x-romaji` 留在 `lexicon.unapplied`（`reason: phoneme`）。写法不对的报在 `unapplied`（`reason: phoneme_format`，`problem` 是 `term`、`count` 或 `form`），`dub` 的文字输出按字母表说哪里不对。库里是 `lexiconRules(lexicon, { phonemeMarkers })`；这个声明写在 `vocab/capabilities.json` 和 `vocab/capability-signatures.md` 里，`adapter new`、`adapter check` 都认它。音素标签用来定名字、生僻词、外来词的读法；标的读法和上下文里一个常见词冲突时，读数里模型照常见词念（`models/scene3.md`「音素标签」）。`references/make-pack-details.md`「读音词表」写了同音字的 `<alias>` 怎么改成 `<phoneme>`，和标签要花的钱。

Converting a voice made before voice packs (`convertLegacyVoice`) lists its use words in `dropped.useCases`, and its one segment `default` has the tag `default`. `clean-room` no longer takes `--scenes`, and `--attributes` with `scenes` is refused (`clean_room_input_invalid`, field `attributes.scenes`). / **场景标签和场景词表去掉了**：删了 `vocab/scenes.json`（12 个场景），连同 `direction.scenes`（`make-pack --direction` 不收）、库里的 `lookupScene`、`MAX_SCENES`、`scenesOfUseCases` 和选项 `sceneUseCases`。音色包以前的老声音转成包（`convertLegacyVoice`）时，用途词列进 `dropped.useCases`，那一段 `default` 的标签是 `default`。`clean-room` 不再收 `--scenes`，`--attributes` 里有 `scenes` 就报错（`clean_room_input_invalid`，`field` 是 `attributes.scenes`）。
- **Speed and spelling marks are no longer markup.** The markup vocabulary is `pause` and `voice` (`vocab/markup.json`); `[slow]`, `[fast]`, `[emphasis]` and `[spell]` written in a script are cues now, sent to the model as written. Speed comes only from `--speed`, a `.json` item's `speed` or a setup's `speed`; stage directions such as `（慢慢地）` no longer set it either, they are cues too. `tools/markup.mjs` no longer gives spans a `speed` or `join`, and the `markup` command no longer takes `--default-speed`. / **语速和拼读不再是台本标记**：台本标记的词表只剩 `pause` 和 `voice`（`vocab/markup.json`）；台本里写的 `[slow]`、`[fast]`、`[emphasis]`、`[spell]` 现在是提示，原样交给模型。语速只由 `--speed`、`.json` 台本一项的 `speed` 或设定的 `speed` 给；`（慢慢地）` 这类舞台说明也不再改语速，同样是提示。`tools/markup.mjs` 的 span 不再有 `speed`、`join`，`markup` 命令不再收 `--default-speed`。
- **How a line is said goes only in the text** (protocol core 3.0.0-beta.1). Synthesis has no parameter for it: `instructions`, `cues` and `continues` are gone (capability vocabulary 2.0), and so are the declarations `style-instruction`, `style-cues`, `pause-in-text` and `cue-in-text`; the declarations are now `phoneme-in-text`, `scene-in-request` and `seed-in-request`. `dub` no longer sends the pack's dubbing guidance: whoever writes the script follows it and writes cues, and `guidance.sent` in the plan and `dub.json` is always false. An adapter that still declares a removed name loads, and `adapter check` reports it (`capability_unknown`). / **怎么说只写在文字里**（协议核心 3.0.0-beta.1）：合成没有交「怎么说」的参数了：`instructions`、`cues`、`continues` 去掉了（能力词表 2.0），声明 `style-instruction`、`style-cues`、`pause-in-text`、`cue-in-text` 也去掉了；现在的声明是 `phoneme-in-text`、`scene-in-request`、`seed-in-request`。`dub` 不再把包的配音指引交给模型：写台本的人照它写提示，规划和 `dub.json` 里的 `guidance.sent` 恒为 false。还声明着已删名字的适配器照样加载，`adapter check` 报出来（`capability_unknown`）。
- **`dub` no longer picks a reference by emotion.** The emotion, style and energy of a line no longer choose its reference, and `dub` no longer asks `text-generation` to judge them; they reach the model as cues. A pick says `by`: `reference-tag`, `setup` or `default`; the 2.x `rule`, `wanted`, `sources`, `matched`, `missed` and `judgeFailed` are gone, as are the plan's `judge` and `dub`'s option `maxPromptTokens`. An adapter's `limits.maxPromptTokens` is still accepted, but no command uses it. / **`dub` 不再按情绪挑参考**：一句的情绪、风格、能量不再决定用哪段参考，`dub` 也不再请 `text-generation` 判断它们；它们写成提示交给模型。挑参考的结果写 `by`：`reference-tag`、`setup`、`default` 之一；2.x 的 `rule`、`wanted`、`sources`、`matched`、`missed`、`judgeFailed` 去掉了，规划的 `judge` 和 `dub` 的选项 `maxPromptTokens` 也去掉了。适配器的 `limits.maxPromptTokens` 照样认，只是没有命令用它。
- **Cues go to the model as written.** Square brackets in a script other than `[pause …]` and `[voice:…]` are cues (`[whispering]`, `[laughs]`, `[小声]`) and go into the request where they are written, unchanged. Stage directions, any `（…）`, `(…)`, `【…】` or `*…*` of up to 30 characters, become cues of the same words in the same place (`（耳语）` gives `[耳语]`), words the vocabulary does not know included (`（画外音）` gives `[画外音]`, listed in `pick.directions` with `recognized: false`); 2.x dropped those. A direction with a pause word is only a pause, even if it also names an emotion. A line holding only a cue passes it to the start of what the same voice says next, or, if nothing follows, to the end of what came before. A cue lasts only within its request: after a pause long enough to cut the request, write it again. / **提示原样交给模型**：台本里除了 `[pause …]`、`[voice:…]`，方括号里的都是提示（`[whispering]`、`[laughs]`、`[小声]`），原样写进请求里它所在的位置。舞台说明（30 个字以内的 `（…）`、`(…)`、`【…】`、`*…*`）换成同样字的提示，留在原位（`（耳语）` 是 `[耳语]`），词表认不出的也一样（`（画外音）` 是 `[画外音]`，`pick.directions` 里记着 `recognized: false`）；2.x 把它们丢掉。括号里有停顿的词的只是停顿，写了情绪也一样。只写了提示的一行，提示接到同一个声音接着说的话的开头，后面没有就接在前面那段的末尾。提示只在它的请求里管用：长到在那里切开请求的停顿后面，要接着那样说就再写一次。
- **`dub --reference-tag`.** `--reference-tag <tag>` uses the reference with that tag in every pack, and `--reference-tag <pack>=<tag>` (repeatable) in one pack; a tag a pack lacks is refused before anything is paid, dry runs included (`reference_tag_unknown`, listing the pack's tags by language). One `dub` uses one tag per pack: to switch partway, dub the parts separately and join them with `mix`. / **`dub --reference-tag`**：`--reference-tag <标签>` 每个包都用这个标签的参考，`--reference-tag <包>=<标签>`（可重复）点某一个包的；包里没有这个标签，花钱之前就报，试运行也报（`reference_tag_unknown`，按语言列出那个包有哪些标签）。一次 `dub` 一个包只用一个标签：中途要换，分几次配，再用 `mix` 接起来。
- **Each line uses the reference of its own language.** The reference is the one with the tag the caller names, else the setup's `referenceTag`, else the default segment of the line's language, picked once per line. Where the language changes between lines and the pack has a default for each, the request is cut there (`language_cut`); a part in another language inside a line follows the line's segment (`followsLine`). A line in a language the pack has no segment for uses the main language's default segment, and a tag missing in the line's language is taken from the main language, then from the first segment with that tag; those picks say `crossLanguage: true`, and every pick names the segment's `tag`. / **每句用它自己语言的参考**：参考取调用方点名的标签的那段，没有取设定的 `referenceTag`，再没有取这句语言的默认段，每句挑一次。行与行之间换了语言、包里两种语言都有默认段的，请求在那里切开（`language_cut`）；一行里另一种语言的一截跟着这一行的段（`followsLine`）。包里没有这句的语言，用主语言的默认段；定了标签、这种语言里没有这个标签的，用主语言里这个标签的段，再没有用排在最前的那段；这几种 `pick.crossLanguage` 是 true，每次挑参考都在 `pick.tag` 写用的段的标签。
- **An opening cue carries the caller's and the setup's values.** Emotion, style, energy and note (`--emotion`, `--style`, `--energy`, `--instructions`, or the setup's) make one cue at the front of each request, such as `[angry, whisper, low energy, a slight Sichuan accent]`, each item from the caller or else the setup; a request whose script already opens with a cue gets none. The plan and `dub.json` record it as `openingCue`. In 2.x, `--instructions` replaced the pack's guidance sent to the model. / **开头的提示带上调用方和设定给的值**：情绪、风格、能量、说明（`--emotion`、`--style`、`--energy`、`--instructions`，或者设定里的）合成一条提示，写在每个请求的最前面，例如 `[angry, whisper, low energy, 带一点四川口音]`，每一项先取调用方给的，没有再取设定的；台本在开头已经写了提示的请求不加。规划和 `dub.json` 记在 `openingCue`。2.x 的 `--instructions` 是代替包里的配音指引交给模型的。
- **`dub` changes the speed after synthesis.** New: `dub --speed`, `speed` on an item of a `.json` script, and the library's `speed`; they, or else the setup's `speed`, are applied to the audio with ffmpeg, keeping the pitch, and recorded in `unit.speedChange`, after which `unit.speed` is null so `mix` does not change it again. Speeds are kept within 0.5 to 2 (`SPEED_LIMITS` in `lib/mix.mjs`), a limit also new to `mix`: a speed outside it is applied at the nearest limit, and `mix` reports `wanted` and `limit` in `speedChanged`. A line with a target duration ignores the speed. Without ffmpeg the audio is handed over as made, with `speed_not_applied`, and the speed is left for `mix`. / **`dub` 合成以后变速**：新加了 `dub --speed`、`.json` 台本一项的 `speed` 和库的 `speed`；它们给的语速，没有再取设定的 `speed`，用 ffmpeg 变速，保持音高，记在 `unit.speedChange`，变过的 `unit.speed` 是 null，`mix` 不再变。语速限在 0.5 到 2 倍（`lib/mix.mjs` 的 `SPEED_LIMITS`），`mix` 也是这一版才有这个上下限：超出的按最近的那一头变，`mix` 在 `speedChanged` 里写 `wanted` 和 `limit`。给了目标时长的一句不用语速。没有 ffmpeg 就照原样交出，有 `speed_not_applied`，语速留给 `mix` 变。
- **Lines go into one request when they fit.** Consecutive lines of one voice in the same chapter, with the same reference, speed and opening cue, and no `dub` script in the pack, go into one request when they fit; `--no-scene` keeps each line its own request. / **放得下的几行并成一个请求**：同一个声音、同一章、参考和语速和开头的提示都一样、包里没有 `dub` 脚本的相邻几行，放得下就并成一个请求；`--no-scene` 时一行一个请求。
- **Short pauses are written into the request.** A pause up to `maxPauseInRequestMs` (750 ms unless the adapter declares `limits.maxPauseInRequestMs`) is written in the text with the measured writing that comes closest (nothing, a comma, `[pause]` …); a longer one cuts the request there and is left as silence for `mix`. Each pause is compared on its own, not added up; `maxPauseInTextMs` given to the library is refused (`dub_input_invalid`). `pauses_in_request` counts the joins. After text ending in Chinese or Japanese characters or their full-width punctuation, the writings take no spaces (`，`, `[pause]`); after other text, Korean included, they take spaces: a comma followed by one, and `[pause]` with one on each side. / **短停顿写进请求**：不超过 `maxPauseInRequestMs`（适配器没声明 `limits.maxPauseInRequestMs` 就是 750 毫秒）的停顿，用量过的写法里出来最接近的那种写在文字里（什么都不加、逗号、`[pause]`……）；长过它的在那里切开，留给 `mix` 插静音。每一处各比各的，不加起来比；给库的 `maxPauseInTextMs` 报 `dub_input_invalid`。`pauses_in_request` 说有几处并了进去。前面以汉字、假名或它们的全角标点收尾的，用不带空格的写法（`，`、`[pause]`）；别的（韩文也算）带空格：逗号后面空一格，`[pause]` 前后各空一格。
- **Text too long for one request is cut at boundaries first.** With `--max-chars` or the adapter's `limits.maxChars`, `dub` cuts first where a line holds only a pause, then between lines, and inside a line between sentences; a sentence that alone does not fit is cut at a comma or similar mark, and failing that wherever it must, never inside a cue. `unit.cutBefore` and `cut_to_fit` say where. / **一个请求放不下，先在边界切**：给了 `--max-chars` 或适配器的 `limits.maxChars` 时，先在只写着停顿的一行切，再在行与行之间，一行自己放不下才在它的句子之间切，一句还放不下的在逗号一类的地方切，再不行硬切（不切开提示）；`unit.cutBefore` 和 `cut_to_fit` 写明切在哪。
- **A cut gets a breath.** Where `dub` cuts by itself between lines or between sentences and the script wrote no pause there, the part after the cut gets `pauseBeforeMs` of 540 ms (`CUT_PAUSE_MS`), which `mix` inserts as silence, and `unit.cutPause` says whether the cut is between lines (`paragraph`) or between sentences (`sentence`). A cut inside a sentence gets none, and with `--no-scene` none is added between lines. / **切口留一口气**：`dub` 自己在行与行之间、句子之间切开、台本在切口没写停顿的，切口后面那一段的 `pauseBeforeMs` 记 540 毫秒（`CUT_PAUSE_MS`），`mix` 照它插静音；`unit.cutPause` 写切在行与行之间（`paragraph`）还是句子之间（`sentence`）。切在一句话中间的不加；`--no-scene` 时行与行之间不加。
- **The same seed for every request.** Where the environment declares `seed-in-request`, every `voice-cloning` and `text-to-dialogue` request gets one fixed seed, recorded in `unit.seed`, so the same request comes out the same. `--only` on a line that came out before in the same `--out` adds one to the seed it was made with (`seedChange`), so asking again gives a new take; if that new take failed, the next `--only` adds one to the failed take's seed instead of going back. Lines carried over keep their seed and `seedChange`. / **每个请求同一个 seed**：环境声明了 `seed-in-request`，每个 `voice-cloning`、`text-to-dialogue` 请求都给同一个固定的 seed，记在 `unit.seed`，同样的请求配出来一样。同一个 `--out` 里做成过的一句，`--only` 再配时 seed 取配它用的加 1（`seedChange`），换一条；换了 seed 没配成的，下一次 `--only` 接着在那一次的上面加 1，不退回去。沿用的句子带着它的 seed 和 `seedChange`。
- **Running again does not pay twice.** When `--out` holds a `dub.json` from an earlier run, a line that came out and whose file is still there is kept if this run would send the same request, matched by content, not by number (`carriedOver`, note `carried_over`). `dub.json` is rewritten after each line, so a run that is killed leaves one marked `dub_unfinished`, which `mix` refuses (`mix_incomplete`); the same command then picks up where it stopped. A dry run with the same `--out` shows what would be kept. Carrying over compares the spoken text, the packs and their versions, the target duration, the speed, the opening cue and the reference, but not the adapter or the model: after switching either, use a new `--out` or `--only`. With `--only`, the lines it does not name keep their previous file and reference, even if this run would pick another. Kept files are renamed to this run's numbers. / **再跑不重复花钱**：`--out` 里有上一次的 `dub.json` 时，做成了、文件还在的一句，这一次要发的请求一样就沿用，按内容认，不按序号（`carriedOver`，注意 `carried_over`）。每做完一句就重写一次 `dub.json`，跑到一半被杀，留下的是一份标着 `dub_unfinished` 的，`mix` 不收（`mix_incomplete`）；再跑同一条命令接着做。试运行给同一个 `--out`，标出能沿用的。沿用比的是要念的话、包和版本、目标时长、语速、开头的提示和参考段，不比适配器和模型：换了适配器或模型，换一个 `--out`，或者用 `--only`。给了 `--only` 时，没点到的句子沿用上一次的文件和参考段，哪怕这一次会挑别的段。沿用的文件按这一次的序号改名。
- **What a `dub.json` unit holds.** `unit.spoken` is now the text sent to the model before the opening cue is put in front: the cues where they are written, short pauses in their measured writing; in 2.x it was the words without markup, and the text sent was in `unit.ssml`. `unit.ssml`, `unit.join`, `unit.instructions` and `unit.cues` are gone. A pack script's `text` hook gets this text, cues included. `unit.pick` is `{ segmentId, tag, by, crossLanguage, followsLine?, language, directions }`. / **`dub.json` 的一句里有什么**：`unit.spoken` 现在是交给模型的文字（开头的提示还没加上）：提示在原位，短停顿写成量过的写法；2.x 里它是不带标记的词，发出去的文字在 `unit.ssml`。`unit.ssml`、`unit.join`、`unit.instructions`、`unit.cues` 没有了。包里脚本的 `text` 钩子拿到的就是这份文字，带着提示。`unit.pick` 是 `{ segmentId, tag, by, crossLanguage, followsLine?, language, directions }`。
- **A target duration for a line timed to picture.** `--target-duration-ms` (or `targetDurationMs` on an item of a `.json` script) changes the speed of that item's audio after synthesis so it lasts that long, within the speed limits; an item that would come out as more than one audio is refused before anything is paid, and `target_duration_missed` lists the ones that could not reach it. / **对画面的一句，给目标时长**：`--target-duration-ms`（或 `.json` 台本里一项的 `targetDurationMs`）让这一项合成以后变速到这个长度，不超出变速的上下限；一项会配成不止一段音频的，花钱之前就报，没变到目标的列在 `target_duration_missed`。
- **Timbre drift is measured.** Where the environment has `speaker-verification`, `dub` compares each audio it made (one per request, as the model gave it: before the pack script's `audio` hook and any speed change) with the reference it used; `--min-similarity` marks the ones below it (`speakerSimilarity.drifted`). `dub` does not dub again by itself, and scene requests are not measured. / **量音色漂移**：环境有 `speaker-verification` 时，`dub` 把配出来的每一段音频（一个请求一段，模型给的原样：包里脚本的 `audio` 钩子和变速之前的）和它用的参考比一次；给了 `--min-similarity`，低于它的标出来（`speakerSimilarity.drifted`）。`dub` 不自己重配；一场戏一个请求的不量。
- **Audio that may have stopped short is flagged.** A unit whose audio is under half the length estimated from its words gets `speech_maybe_unfinished`: a model can stop halfway without an error. / **可能没念完的会提醒**：一段音频不到按字数估的一半，有一条 `speech_maybe_unfinished`：模型念到一半停下不一定报错。
- **A speaker's name at the start of a line is flagged.** Lines written as `Name: words`, starting with the name or ID of a given pack, get `speaker_name_in_line`: the name is read aloud and the voice does not change; write `[voice:Name]` instead. / **台词开头写着说话人的名字会提醒**：写成「名字：台词」、开头是给了的某个包的名字或包 ID 的几句，有一条 `speaker_name_in_line`：名字会照念，声音也不换；改成 `[voice:名字]`。
- **Spaces where text is joined.** Joining sentences or lines adds a space unless one side is a Chinese or Japanese character or full-width punctuation, looking past quotes, brackets, ellipses and dashes at the join; Korean now gets a space, as English does. `make-pack` joins sentences into segments the same way. / **接起来的地方放不放空格**：几句、几行接起来时，接的地方有一边是汉字、假名或它们的全角标点就直接接（隔着接口处的引号、括号、省略号、破折号看），别的加一个空格；韩文现在和英文一样加空格。`make-pack` 把句子接成段也照这一条。
- **A scene is one request.** Where the environment has `text-to-dialogue` and declares `scene-in-request`, and several packs are given, a stretch of turns by different speakers goes into one `text-to-dialogue` request and comes out as one audio file, `<number>-scene.<extension>`: one request for the scene instead of one per line (`scene_in_request` counts the scenes and their lines). An environment without both sends no scene requests, and with `--no-scene` (`options.scene: false` in the library) each line is its own request. / **一场戏一个请求**：环境有 `text-to-dialogue`、又声明了 `scene-in-request`，还给了几个包时，几个说话人轮流说的一段话放进一个 `text-to-dialogue` 请求，出一个音频文件 `<序号>-scene.<扩展名>`：一场一个请求，不再一行一个（`scene_in_request` 写有几场、共几行）。两样不全的环境不发一场戏的请求；给了 `--no-scene`（库里是 `options.scene: false`），一行一个请求。
- **One reference per speaker in a scene.** Each speaker uses the reference picked most often across his lines of the scene (a tie goes to the one that appears first); the cues written in each line go inside that turn's text, where they are written, with the opening cue at the front of the turn, so an emotional arc rides on the cues, not on swapping the reference. / **一场戏里每个说话人只用一段参考**：他在这一场各行挑到的参考里次数最多的那一段，并列取先出现的；每一行写的提示放在这一轮的文字里它所在的位置，开头的提示写在这一轮的最前面，情绪起伏靠提示，不靠换参考。
- **Where a scene ends.** A scene is cut at a chapter change, a pause the author wrote, a change of speed, a pack that has a `dub` script of its own, and at the adapter's speaker limit (`limits.maxSpeakers`, none unless the adapter gives one). A stretch with only one speaker is not a scene: it goes to `voice-cloning` and is joined like other lines. A scene the platform says does not fit one request is not cut or sent again: it fails with `scene_too_long` while the other scenes carry on, and the way out is a chapter marker inside it, or `--no-scene`. / **在哪里切开**：换章、作者写的停顿、语速变了、包里自带 `dub` 脚本的包，和适配器声明的说话人上限（`limits.maxSpeakers`，适配器不给就不限）都是切点。只有一个人说话的一段不成场，用 `voice-cloning`，和别的行一样并。平台说一场装不下，不自动切开、也不重发：那一场记成失败（`scene_too_long`），别的场照常配，办法是在里面加一个章节标记，或者用 `--no-scene`。
- **A scene has no audio of its own per line.** One request makes one audio, and the platform gives no boundary or time for each turn; `dub.json` lists the scene as one unit with the speakers' references and every turn's words and cues, and `--only` counts a scene as one number. Use `--no-scene` when each line needs its own file. / **一场戏里每一行没有各自的音频**：一个请求出一个音频，平台没有给每一轮的边界和时间；`dub.json` 里这一场是一个 unit，列着每个说话人的参考和每一轮的话、提示，`--only` 把一场算一个编号。要每行一个文件，用 `--no-scene`。
- **`mix` knows where a scene ends.** A part made of a scene carries `lastSpeakerPack`, the pack of its last turn, so the edge of the next part is judged against the right speaker; parts without it are unchanged. / **`mix` 知道一场戏在哪个说话人那里结束**：由一场戏做成的一段带 `lastSpeakerPack`，是最后一轮的包，下一段接上去的边按它判；没有这一项的段不变。
- **`mix` joins several directories.** `mix <dir>... --out <file.wav>` joins the outputs of several `dub` runs in the order given, the way two neighbouring lines are joined; numbers run on from the first directory, and the report's `directories` gives each one's `{ directory, units, first, last }`. Every directory is checked before any audio is read, and the first incomplete one is named in `details.directory`. / **`mix` 接几个目录**：`mix <目录>... --out <文件.wav>` 把几次 `dub` 的输出按给的次序接成一个文件，接法同一个目录里相邻的两句；号数从第一个目录接着往下数，报告的 `directories` 写每个目录的 `{ directory, units, first, last }`。先把每个目录都查过再读音频，第一个不完整的目录写在 `details.directory`。
- **`convert` picks a reference by tag.** `convert --reference-tag <tag>` (given once) uses the reference with that tag; without it, the default setup's `referenceTag`, then the default segment of the language given with `--language` or by the setup. `--emotion`, `--style` and `--energy` are gone. / **`convert` 按标签挑参考**：`convert --reference-tag <标签>`（只给一次）用这个标签的参考；不给，取默认设定的 `referenceTag`，再是 `--language` 或设定给的语言的默认段。`--emotion`、`--style`、`--energy` 去掉了。
- **Material gives each segment a `language` and a `tag`.** A segment's `.json`, or the input `.json`, writes `language` and `tag` beside `text`, and `--language` gives every segment a default. The old `labels`, per segment or for the input, are refused before anything is paid (`make_pack_input_invalid`). `make-pack` no longer tags emotion and style by listening or measures energy, and the hints `emotion_untagged`, `emotion_missing` and `energy_missing` are gone. The report gives each reference `{ language, languageSource, tag, tagSource, renamedFrom? }`; an evaluation set saved with `--save-evaluation` labels references `{ language, tag }` and holdouts `{ language }`. / **素材给每段写 `language` 和 `tag`**：段的 `.json` 或输入的 `.json` 在 `text` 旁边写 `language` 和 `tag`，`--language` 给所有段一个默认。旧写法的 `labels`（每段的、输入的）花钱之前就报 `make_pack_input_invalid`。`make-pack` 不再听着标情绪、风格，也不再量能量，提示 `emotion_untagged`、`emotion_missing`、`energy_missing` 去掉了。报告里每段参考写 `{ language, languageSource, tag, tagSource, renamedFrom? }`；`--save-evaluation` 存下的评测集，参考写 `{ language, tag }`，留出录音写 `{ language }`。
- **Untagged references get a name, and `--retag` renames.** A reference without a tag is named in the same call that annotates it (below), in the skeleton's language, after its most marked emotion, age or state; failing that, it takes its `id`. Within a language a clash gets a number ("angry 2"), and a tag someone gave wins over a model's name or an id. `--retag <segment>=<tag>` (with `--previous`, repeatable) renames a reference of the previous version; a version that only renames is still a new version, with `retagged` in its changes. `tagSource` is `material`, `model`, `id` or `previous`. / **没给标签的参考由做包起名，`--retag` 改名**：没给标签的参考，在给它标提示标注的那一次调用里（见下）顺带起名，用骨架的语言，说它的情绪、年龄或状态里最突出的一种；起不成用段名（`id`）。同一种语言里撞名的加序号（「愤怒 2」），有人给过的标签优先于模型起的名字和段名。`--retag <段名>=<标签>`（要给 `--previous`，可重复）改上一版一段参考的标签；只改名也发得成新版本，变化里有 `retagged`。`tagSource` 是 `material`、`model`、`id`、`previous` 之一。
- **`make-pack` listens to mark cues.** Where the environment has `audio-text-to-text`, each reference new in this version whose material brings no cues is annotated in one call: the model hears it, writes the opening words it heard (checked against the transcript like the blind listen; a failed check is not retried, only a service error is asked again, up to 3 tries), and gives each cue with the words that follow it; the cues are put into the transcript, whose words do not change. A reference that fails keeps only its transcript, and the report says which and why (`steps.annotation`); holdouts are not annotated, and the call is in the cost estimate. / **`make-pack` 听着标提示**：环境有 `audio-text-to-text` 时，这一版新挑进参考集、素材没带提示标注的每段调一次：模型听这一段，写出它听到的开头几个词（和逐字稿对，同盲听；对不上不重试，服务暂时出错才再问，最多 3 次），再给每处提示和它后面紧跟的几个字；提示按位置插进逐字稿，逐字稿的字一个不改。没标上的段只存逐字稿，报告写明是哪几段、为什么（`steps.annotation`）；留出录音不标，这次调用算在费用估算里。
- **Material can bring its own cues.** A segment's `annotatedText` in the material is kept, and that segment is not sent to the model; its words outside the cues must match `text`, and without `text` the transcript is taken from it and not transcribed. Where a cue is taken out to make that transcript, it goes with the spaces around it, and the two sides, looked at past quotes, brackets, ellipses and dashes, join directly where one is full-width punctuation, both are Chinese or Japanese characters, or the second starts with an English closing mark (`. , ! ? ; : ) ] }`); otherwise one space is left if there was one, or if both sides are touching Latin, Greek, Cyrillic or Korean letters or digits (`That's enough [sigh].` gives `That's enough.`, `I told you[sigh]already` gives `I told you already`). To change the cues of a reference from the previous version, drop it and give it again under a new `id`. / **素材可以自带提示标注**：素材里段的 `annotatedText` 照给的存，这一段不交给模型标；提示以外的字要和 `text` 一样，不写 `text` 的，逐字稿取它去掉提示的那句，不转写。这样推逐字稿时，提示连同两边的空白一起去掉，两边这样接（隔着引号、括号、省略号、破折号看）：有一边是全角标点、两边都是汉字或假名、或者后一截开头是英文的收尾标点（`. , ! ? ; : ) ] }`），直接接；别的，提示两边原来有空白就留一个，两边都是拉丁、希腊、西里尔字母、韩文或数字又紧挨着也留一个（`That's enough [sigh].` 是 `That's enough.`，`I told you[sigh]already` 是 `I told you already`）。要改上一版一段参考的提示标注，`--drop` 那一段，换一个段名重新交。
- **A listening model picks each language's default segment.** For a language with no default named with `--default-segment` (repeatable, one per language) and none carried over, a model that hears audio picks, from up to 8 candidates, the most natural segment, one that sounds like talking to someone; its opening words are checked, its reason goes to the report's `why`, and `unpicked` says why when it did not pick. The call is in the cost estimate. Without such a model, or when it did not pick, the segment most like the others by speaker similarity is used, or else the one first by priority. Regenerated (`synthetic`) segments are candidates like recordings. / **听音频的模型给每种语言挑默认段**：一种语言没用 `--default-segment` 点名（可以给几次，每种语言一段）、也没有沿用的，请能听音频的模型从最多 8 段候选里挑最自然、像在跟人说话的一段；它写的开头几个词要对得上，理由写进报告的 `why`，没挑成的写在 `unpicked`。这次调用算在费用估算里。没有这种模型、或者它没挑成的，有说话人相似度就取和其余参考最像的一段，再没有就按优先级。重生成的段（`synthetic`）和录音一样是候选。
- **`--card-line` voices the line for the card.** `make-pack --card-line <file.txt>` (`cardLine` in the library) voices a line written from the character card with the new pack, the way `dub` would with no setup and no reference named, in one request counted in the dry-run plan and the cost estimate. A line `dub` would send as more than one request is refused before anything is paid (`make_pack_input_invalid`, field `cardLine`). If voicing fails, the pack is made without it and the report says `card_line_failed`, or `card_line_halted` when the credentials or the account were refused (fix those, do not retry); a line longer than the card holds gets `card_line_trimmed`, a model change `card_line_model_changed`, and a pack without a line `card_line_missing`, which says the same for recorded and designed voices: the card plays the main language's default segment. / **`--card-line` 配卡片试听那一句**：`make-pack --card-line <文件.txt>`（库里是 `cardLine`）用新包配一句照角色卡写的台词，照 `dub` 不点名设定、不点名参考时的规矩，一个请求，算在试运行的规划和费用估算里。`dub` 会配成不止一个请求的，花钱之前就报（`make_pack_input_invalid`，`field` 是 `cardLine`）。配了没成，包照做，报告有 `card_line_failed`；凭据或账户被拒的是 `card_line_halted`（先处理凭据或账户，不要重试）。比卡片能放的长有 `card_line_trimmed`，换了模型有 `card_line_model_changed`，没给这一句有 `card_line_missing`，录音做的和设计出来的声音说的都是同一句：卡片上放的是主语言的默认段。
- **The card line stays until it is given again.** A new version without `--card-line` carries the previous line and its audio as they are, with no capability called (`cardLine.status` `carried`). The report compares it with how `dub` would voice it now and lists what differs in `cardLine.reasons` (`reference_changed`, `request_text_changed`, `speed_changed`, `not_one_request`, `default_segment_changed`), with the hint `card_line_stale`. Where the environment declares `seed-in-request`, giving the same line again when the request would be the same uses the previous seed plus one, so a misread line gets a new take. A version's changes say `cardLineChanged`, `cardLineDropped` or `cardLineFailed`. / **卡片那一句不再交就一直沿用**：发新版本不给 `--card-line`，上一版的那一句连音频原样带过来，一次能力都不调（`cardLine.status` 是 `carried`）。报告照现在 `dub` 的配法比一比，对不上的写进 `cardLine.reasons`（`reference_changed`、`request_text_changed`、`speed_changed`、`not_one_request`、`default_segment_changed`），提示 `card_line_stale`。环境声明了 `seed-in-request` 时，交同样的一句、请求也和上一版一样，seed 取上一版的加 1，念错的那一句能配出新的一条。版本的变化里写 `cardLineChanged`、`cardLineDropped`、`cardLineFailed`。
- **A design source is not carried once its segments are gone.** A new version without `--design-source` keeps the previous one only while at least one previous segment stays; when all are dropped it has none, with `design_source_dropped` in `report.notes` and `designSourceDropped` in the changes. With `--audition`, `design_source_missing` is no longer left out just because `--previous` is given: only when the previous design source is carried. / **段都去掉了，设计来源不再沿用**：发新版本不给 `--design-source`，上一版的段至少留着一段才沿用上一版的；全去掉了就没有设计来源，`report.notes` 里有 `design_source_dropped`，变化里有 `designSourceDropped`。给了 `--audition` 的，`design_source_missing` 不再因为给了 `--previous` 就不提醒，只有沿用了上一版的设计来源才不提醒。
- **A long recording is transcribed per segment, not per sentence.** `--recording` cuts sentences, joins them in order into 5–20 s segments and transcribes each segment once: a short sentence has no context, and a scrap with no one speaking can come back as words. Counts, the cost estimate and the dry-run plan count segments (`report.counts.input`, `plan.segments.input`). Material can mark pieces as sentences (`sentence: { bodySec, pauseSec }`) to be joined the same way; when every sentence of a segment has `text`, the segment's text is theirs joined, and it is not transcribed. Segments are not split by language: a segment's language is `--language`, else what its transcription reports, else what its text shows; to separate languages, cut the recording yourself and give each piece its `language`. / **长录音按段转写，不按句**：`--recording` 切出句子，按原来的先后并成 5–20 秒的段，一段转写一次：短句没有上下文，没人说话的一小块还会转写出一句话来。段数、费用估算和试运行的规划都按段算（`report.counts.input`、`plan.segments.input`）。素材可以把几段标成句子（`sentence: { bodySec, pauseSec }`），照样并成段；段里每一句都写了 `text` 的，段的文字就是它们接起来，不转写。并段不按语言分开：段的语言是 `--language` 给的，没给就用这一段转写报的语言，再没有就从整段的文字认；要按语言分开，先自己切好，每段写上 `language` 当素材交。
- **A transcription the provider blocks drops only that segment.** `provider_blocked` from `automatic-speech-recognition` is not retried: the segment is not used (`transcription_blocked`, counted in `steps.transcription.blocked`; its `details` are `{ error, attempts }`, as for `transcription_failed`), and the others carry on. `prepareLongRecording` does the same when it transcribes: the segment is dropped, `report.transcription.blocked` counts it, its `transcription` is `blocked`, and a note `transcription_blocked` lists it; in 2.x `provider_blocked` stopped it. An adapter whose service sometimes passes the same recording when asked again may report the block as transient, with `details.problem: 'provider_blocked'` (and `provider`, `reply` when it can tell them); `make-pack` asks again as for any hiccup, and when the tries run out and the last error is still a block, the segment is `transcription_blocked` too, with the hint to add the transcript or use another transcription (in 2.x it was `transcription_failed`, with a hint to redo it later); `prepareLongRecording` drops it as `blocked` the same way. In a long recording's `report.segments`, segments that failed or were blocked have `attempts`. / **服务商拦下的转写只丢那一段**：`automatic-speech-recognition` 抛 `provider_blocked` 不重试：那一段用不上（`transcription_blocked`，数在 `steps.transcription.blocked`；`details` 是 `{ error, attempts }`，同 `transcription_failed`），别的段照做。`prepareLongRecording` 转写时也一样：丢掉那一段，数在 `report.transcription.blocked`，它的 `transcription` 是 `blocked`，说明 `transcription_blocked` 列出是哪几段；2.x 碰到 `provider_blocked` 整个停下。同一段再问有时就过的服务，适配器可以把被拦下照暂时出错报，在 `details.problem` 写 `provider_blocked`（认得出就再带 `provider`、`reply`）：`make-pack` 照暂时出错再问，问满了次数、最后一次还是被拦下，那一段也记成 `transcription_blocked`，提示说补上逐字文本或者换一个转写（2.x 记成 `transcription_failed`，提示稍后重做）；`prepareLongRecording` 也照样丢掉那一段，记成 `blocked`。长录音的 `report.segments` 里没转成的段（`failed`、`blocked`）带 `attempts`。
- **The listening check accepts other writings of the same words.** Katakana and hiragana count as the same, and in text with Chinese characters so do Arabic numerals and their reading in characters (94 and 九十四; with kana, the Japanese reading, 100 and 百; thousands separators ignored); both the folded and the plain comparison are made and the lower error rate is used, so folding never fails a segment that the plain comparison passes (`openingErrorRate`). / **收音校验认同一句话的别的写法**：片假名和平假名算一样；有汉字的话里，阿拉伯数字和它的汉字读法也算一样（94 和九十四；有假名的照日文读，100 和百；千分位逗号不算）；折过的和原样的都比，取错误率小的，所以不会比原样比更容易作废（`openingErrorRate`）。
- **Nothing usable stops before transcribing.** When it can be seen before transcribing that no segment can be used (all screened out, or no transcript and no `automatic-speech-recognition` in the environment), `make-pack` stops there, dry runs included (exit 1): `make_pack_no_material`, with `details.unused` (reasons and counts) and, when the missing transcription is the reason, `details.missingCapability`. With `--recording` or `--audition`, no `automatic-speech-recognition`, no `--previous` and no material with a transcript, it does not call separation or VAD either. / **一段都用不上，转写之前就停**：转写之前就看得出一段都用不上时（都被筛掉了，或者没有逐字文本、环境又没有 `automatic-speech-recognition`），`make-pack` 在那里停下，试运行也报（退出 1）：`make_pack_no_material`，带 `details.unused`（原因和段数）；缺转写的另带 `details.missingCapability`。给了 `--recording` 或 `--audition`、环境没有 `automatic-speech-recognition`、不是发新版本、素材里也没有一段带逐字文本时，分离人声和 VAD 也不调。
- **Mistakes in the input are reported before separation and VAD.** With `--recording` or `--audition`, what the input alone shows (the input and options, the previous version, `--retag`, `--drop`, the version number, named default segments, setups' `referenceTag`, the manifest rules, whether the card line fits one request) is checked before any paid step. / **输入的错，在分离人声、VAD 之前就报**：给了 `--recording` 或 `--audition` 时，只看输入就查得出的错（输入和选项、上一版、`--retag`、`--drop`、版本号、点名的默认段、设定的 `referenceTag`、清单的规矩、卡片那一句配不配得成一个请求）在任何花钱的步骤之前查。
- **A sample line that is not a string is an input error.** It is `make_pack_input_invalid`, with `field` `sampleLines[<n>]`, before anything is paid; 2.x reported `manifest_invalid`. / **不是字符串的示例台词是输入的错**：花钱之前报 `make_pack_input_invalid`，`field` 是 `sampleLines[<序号>]`；2.x 报的是 `manifest_invalid`。
- **Trial failures say what kind they are.** In `make-pack`'s text output, the trial syntheses (the ones that measure fidelity) that did not come out are told apart: those that failed for a service error, those refused for their text, and those done but not retrieved (already billed, not called again). / **试合成没成的分开说**：`make-pack` 的文字输出里，量还原度的试合成没成的分开写：服务出错的、文字被拒的、做完了没取回的（计过费，不再调）。
- **Fidelity trials use each language's default segment.** Each holdout is synthesized with the default segment of its language as the reference, with no setup and no `referenceTag`; in 2.x each was picked by its own labels. Holdouts are grouped by language and the tag the material gave (a model's name, an `id` used as a tag and the previous version's holdouts count as no tag), instead of language and emotion. / **量还原度的试合成用每种语言的默认段**：每条留出录音用它那种语言的默认段当参考，不用设定，也不用 `referenceTag`；2.x 是每条按它自己的标签挑。留出录音按「语言加素材给的音色基底标签」分组（模型起的名字、段名兜底的、上一版的留出录音算没有标签），不再按「语言加情绪」。
- **`text-to-dialogue` takes `turns`, and `scene-in-request` declares it.** Each turn is `{ speaker, text }`, given instead of `text`; the turn's cues, its opening cue included, are written inside its `text` where they take effect. Malformed turns are `capability_input_invalid` (`problem` `text_and_turns` when both are given, `speaker_out_of_range` for a speaker past the list). `expressiveness` may be left out. A platform that says a scene does not fit throws with `details.problem` `too_long_for_one_request` and `textRejected`, not `transient`. `seed-in-request` declares that synthesis takes a `seed`. / **`text-to-dialogue` 收 `turns`，由 `scene-in-request` 声明**：每一轮是 `{ speaker, text }`，和 `text` 二选一；这一轮的提示（连开头的提示）写在它的 `text` 里生效的位置。轮写错了报 `capability_input_invalid`（两样都给了 `problem` 是 `text_and_turns`，说话人越界是 `speaker_out_of_range`）。`expressiveness` 可以不给。平台说一场装不下，抛的错 `details.problem` 写 `too_long_for_one_request`，标 `textRejected`，不标 `transient`。`seed-in-request` 声明合成收 `seed`。
- **An adapter can say it changed the model.** Any capability's result can carry `modelChange: { from, reason }`, with `model` the one actually used. What reads it for now is `dub`'s `voice-cloning` and `text-to-dialogue` calls (so also `make-pack`'s trial syntheses and card line): a malformed one there is `capability_result_invalid` (`problem` `model_change_invalid`) and stops the run, except that a card line voiced with it is recorded as failed. `dub` records it on each line and says which lines changed; `make-pack --card-line` reports it. / **适配器可以报换了模型**：任何一项能力的返回里都可以带 `modelChange: { from, reason }`，`model` 写实际用的。现在读它的是 `dub` 调的 `voice-cloning`、`text-to-dialogue`（`make-pack` 的试合成和卡片那一句也走这里）：写错了报 `capability_result_invalid`（`problem` 是 `model_change_invalid`），停下来；只有配卡片那一句时不停，记成没配成。`dub` 每一句记着它，并说哪几句换了；`make-pack --card-line` 也报。
- **Work done but not retrieved is marked.** An error whose work finished and was billed, but whose result could not be fetched, carries `details.unretrieved: true` and `details.retrieve`, a sentence saying how to fetch it (a command, a task number; no address, no credentials); this replaces the `details.action` `download` and `details.taskRunId` of the 2.x notes. `dub` does not suggest `--only` for those lines and says how to retrieve them; `mix` and `make-pack` say the same. `dub.json` and `dub --json` list those lines in a top-level `unretrieved`, by the numbers `--only` takes, absent when there are none. A later `--only` run in the same `--out` that does not name such a line, and would send it as the same request, keeps it as not retrieved, `retrieve` and all, instead of marking it skipped. / **做完了没取回的要标出来**：服务那头做完、计过费，只是结果没取回来的错，带 `details.unretrieved: true`，再在 `details.retrieve` 里写一句怎么取回（命令、任务号这类线索，不写网址、不写凭据）；代替 2.x 说明里的 `details.action` 为 `download` 和 `details.taskRunId`。`dub` 不叫人对这几句 `--only` 重做，说怎么取回；`mix`、`make-pack` 也这样说。`dub.json` 和 `dub --json` 的输出顶层有 `unretrieved`，列出这几句的序号（和 `--only` 的一样），一句都没有就没有这一项。之后在同一个 `--out` 里用 `--only` 重做别的句子，没点到的这几句，请求和上一次一样的，照旧记成没取回，`retrieve` 还在，不记成 `skipped`。
- **A transcription cut short is not handed back half-written.** An `automatic-speech-recognition` answer that did not finish (cut off, broken midway) is not handed back half-written. When asking again would most likely work (for example the answer hit an output limit set well above a normal transcript), the adapter reports it as transient, so `make-pack` asks again (up to 3 tries) and then drops only that segment (`transcription_failed`) instead of stopping the whole run; when asking again would give the same, it is not marked and `make-pack` stops. An output limit the adapter sets for transcription has to leave room for output that is not shown: some models keep thinking and use a few hundred extra tokens even on a correct answer, and without that room normal answers are cut and asked again. / **转写没正常结束，半截的不交回来**：`automatic-speech-recognition` 的回答被截断、中途出错，半截的文字不交回来。同一段再问多半就成的（例如回答碰到了比正常逐字稿宽得多的输出上限），适配器照暂时出错报（标 `transient`），`make-pack` 就再问（最多 3 次），还不成只丢那一段（`transcription_failed`），不整次停下；再问还是一样的不标，`make-pack` 停下。适配器给转写设的输出上限，要给看不见的输出留够：有的模型思考关不干净，字都对的回答也多用几百个看不见的词元，不留就会把正常的回答也截断，白白再问。
- **Sentence splitting reads past cues.** `tools/sentences.mjs` hides each square-bracket cue before splitting: a cue after a full stop goes with the next sentence, and whether the two join is decided by the word after the cue (`…the line. [whispering] Then…` is now two sentences, `He paused. [sighs] and then…` stays one); the split no longer falls inside the brackets (`你好。[小声]我说`), and a cue with no words after it stays with the sentence before. An English full stop followed by a cue with no space (`end.[sigh]Then`) does not split, as `end.Then` does not. / **分句跳过提示**：`tools/sentences.mjs` 分句之前先把方括号的提示遮住：句末后面的提示跟着后一句，两句接不接看提示后面的字（`…the line. [whispering] Then…` 现在是两句，`He paused. [sighs] and then…` 照旧是一句）；不再从提示中间切开（`你好。[小声]我说`），后面没有字的提示跟着前一句。英文句号后面不空格就接提示（`end.[sigh]Then`）不切，和 `end.Then` 一样。
- **Library changes.** `pickReferences(manifest, lines, options)` is synchronous and takes no `env`. Removed: `lib/segment-tags.mjs` (`tagSegment`, `TAG_PROMPT`, `parseTagAnswer`, `EMOTIONS`, `STYLES`), `combineJudges`, `MAIN_EMOTIONS`, `SETUPS_BASE_MIN`, `tagAll`, `assembleTags`, `assertDefaultUsable` (now `assertDefaultsUsable`), `SPEED` and the `defaultSpeed` option of `tools/markup.mjs`, `maxPauseInTextMsOf` and `maxPromptTokensOf` of `cli/adapters.mjs`, and the SSML pieces of `lib/pause-in-request.mjs` (`renderRun`, `cueTag`, `escapeSsml`, `glue`, `endsMidSentence`, `BREAK_MIN_MS`, `PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_MS`, `PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_MAX_MS`, `JOIN_PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_MS`, `JOIN_PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_SEC`): pauses are no longer written as `<break>`, and a request is wrapped in `<speak>` only for phonemes. `requestsOf` takes `boundary` and `extra`, and its `maxPauseMs` now limits each pause, 750 ms by default instead of none. New: `pauseWriting`, `PAUSE_WRITINGS`, `BOUNDARIES`, `DEFAULT_MAX_PAUSE_IN_REQUEST_MS`, `SPEED_LIMITS`, `changeSpeed`, `CUT_PAUSE_MS`, `DUB_SEED`, `cuesInPlace`, and `languageKey`, `segmentLanguage`, `referenceTagsByLanguage` in `protocol/lib/manifest.mjs`. / **库的变化**：`pickReferences(manifest, lines, options)` 改成同步的，不收 `env`。去掉了：`lib/segment-tags.mjs`（`tagSegment`、`TAG_PROMPT`、`parseTagAnswer`、`EMOTIONS`、`STYLES`）、`combineJudges`、`MAIN_EMOTIONS`、`SETUPS_BASE_MIN`、`tagAll`、`assembleTags`、`assertDefaultUsable`（换成 `assertDefaultsUsable`）、`tools/markup.mjs` 的 `SPEED` 和选项 `defaultSpeed`、`cli/adapters.mjs` 的 `maxPauseInTextMsOf` 和 `maxPromptTokensOf`，以及 `lib/pause-in-request.mjs` 拼 SSML 的那些（`renderRun`、`cueTag`、`escapeSsml`、`glue`、`endsMidSentence`、`BREAK_MIN_MS`、`PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_MS`、`PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_MAX_MS`、`JOIN_PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_MS`、`JOIN_PUNCTUATION_PAUSE_SEC`）：停顿不再写成 `<break>`，只有拼音音素才给请求包一层 `<speak>`。`requestsOf` 多收 `boundary`、`extra`，`maxPauseMs` 改成每一处停顿的上限，默认从不限变成 750 毫秒。新加：`pauseWriting`、`PAUSE_WRITINGS`、`BOUNDARIES`、`DEFAULT_MAX_PAUSE_IN_REQUEST_MS`、`SPEED_LIMITS`、`changeSpeed`、`CUT_PAUSE_MS`、`DUB_SEED`、`cuesInPlace`，`protocol/lib/manifest.mjs` 的 `languageKey`、`segmentLanguage`、`referenceTagsByLanguage`。
- **`adapter check` fixes.** The `--live` command it suggests now runs as printed: the adapter in the first position with its options, and `cli.mjs` by its full path. `adapter new --help` and `adapter check --help` print that subcommand's usage. / **`adapter check` 的修正**：它建议的 `--live` 命令照抄就能跑：适配器放在第一个位置、带着它的参数，`cli.mjs` 写完整路径。`adapter new --help`、`adapter check --help` 打印这个子命令的用法。
- **Dubbing goes by scene.** `SKILL.md` sends each dubbing job to a sub-skill under `scenes/`: `scenes/one-line.md` for a line said to someone in a conversation (and a line timed to picture), `scenes/a-scene.md` for several characters taking turns, `scenes/narration.md` for narration, long text and voice-overs. Each says what fitting means there, how to edit the script, which cues to write, which reference to use and what to check. / **配音按场景走**：`SKILL.md` 把每次配音指到 `scenes/` 下的子 skill：对话里对某个人说的一句（和对画面的一句）是 `scenes/one-line.md`，几个角色轮流说的一场戏是 `scenes/a-scene.md`，旁白、长文和口播是 `scenes/narration.md`。每一份写这个场景里「贴合」指什么、台本怎么改、提示怎么写、用哪段参考、配完查什么。
- **Cues: full, precise, short.** The scene guides, the card line, the markup reference and the model notes ask for a cue wherever the delivery changes, several in one sentence if need be. Each cue is two or three words naming the feeling or the way of saying it there, never a sentence: what the line answers, who it is said to and what it wants stay out of the brackets. How strong a cue is follows what is said at that point; narration follows its content too, in its cues and in the reference picked for a chapter, instead of keeping a narrow range. The model notes, the guides for a line and for a scene, and the card line add that a sound word in a cue may be read out: for a laugh write the way of saying it (`[笑着]`) or `[laughs]`, not the sound itself; neither is shown to make a laugh. / **提示写得充分、细致、简洁**：场景子 skill、卡片那一句、台本标记的说明和模型说明都要求：说法变了就写一个提示，一句里可以几个；每个两三个词，说清这一处的情绪或说法，不写成句子；这一句在回应什么、对谁说、想让对方怎样，不写进方括号。提示的强弱照这一处的内容定；旁白也跟着内容走，提示和整章用的参考都照内容定，不压成一个窄的调子。模型说明、对话里的一句和一场戏的子 skill、卡片那一句还写明：提示里的拟声词可能被念出来，要笑写说法（`[笑着]`）或 `[laughs]`，不写「嘿嘿」这样的字；这两种写法都没测出一定笑得出来。
- **Model notes.** `models/` has one note per model, about the model itself and not any platform: `models/scene3.md` for the model that dubs (cloning, its own voice, scenes) and one for the model that designs voices. Each item gives its source, and what was not tested says so; the sub-skills point to the sections they rely on. Model names now appear only in `models/` and in platform adapters outside the base part; the rest of the base part, its `adapters/` included, names only capabilities. / **模型说明**：`models/` 下一个模型一份，只写模型本身，不绑平台：配音的模型是 `models/scene3.md`（克隆、自带的声音、一场戏），另有一份写设计声音的模型。每条写出处，没测过的写明没测过；子 skill 指到它们依赖的那一节。模型名只出现在 `models/` 和基础部分以外的平台适配器里；基础部分的其余地方（连同 `adapters/`）只写能力名。
- **Choosing a voice.** The order is the user's recording of the character, then the character's own voice in the library, used as a recording, then a designed voice. Borrowing someone else's voice from the library by matching tags is paused: a borrowed recording carries its owner's way of speaking. / **选声音**：次序是使用者给的这个角色的录音，再是库里这个角色本人的声音（当成录音用），都没有就设计一个。按标签借库里别人的声音这一步暂停了：借来的录音带着原主的说话方式。
- **A designed voice is raw material, and emotion lines are no longer cloned.** The audition a design makes is no longer the pack's reference or its default segment: it is unprocessed, flat speech, and a pack cloned from it plays every line flat; cloning emotion lines from it only copied its flat reading. A designed voice now takes two runs of `make-pack` with a `dub` in between, and no new command. The temporary pack holds only the audition. With it, `dub` voices a passage of 10 to 20 seconds written from the character card: the character talking as usual, with a short cue wherever the delivery changes. The take must come from the dubbing model: a take the adapter voiced with another model (`modelChange`) is not used. A take that drops sentences is voiced again once; the check is the final pack's transcript. The final pack holds only that take, as its one reference and default segment; the card line, later dubbing and a stand-in model all clone it. The commands are in `references/make-pack.md` 「设计出来的声音怎么做包」; to try another voice, the new take is `acted-2`, `acted-3` and so on, and the previous segments are dropped. `make-pack --audition` takes default-voice and blended auditions only. The audition text is about 40 Chinese characters or 25 English words, enough for about 10 s said at ease: longer texts were read fast, and clones follow the reference's pace. / **设计出来的声音只当原料，不再克隆情绪台词**：设计出来的试听不再是包的参考，也不再是默认段。它是没处理过的干声，念得平，拿它克隆，每一句都演得平；拿它克隆情绪台词，出来的还是平读。现在设计出来的声音做两次包，中间用 `dub` 配一段，不加新命令。临时的包里只有试听。用它照角色卡配一段 10 到 20 秒的话：角色平常跟人说话的样子，说法变了的地方写一个短提示。那一段要是配音的模型配的：适配器换了模型配的（`modelChange`）不用。少念了句子的重配一次，拿正式的包的逐字稿对。正式的包里只有配出来的这一段，它是唯一的参考，也是默认段；卡片那一句、之后的配音、临时换上的模型，克隆的都是它。命令见 `references/make-pack.md`「设计出来的声音怎么做包」。换一个时，新的一段叫 `acted-2`、`acted-3`，依次往下，上一版的段都去掉。`make-pack --audition` 只收默认音色、融合出来的试听。试听文本写约 10 秒能从容念完的量，中文约 40 字，英文约 25 个词：字一多就念得快，克隆又照着参考的节奏念。
- **Every voice gets a card line written from the character card.** Recorded and designed voices alike: one or two sentences, about 5 to 8 s, said in the card's situation to whoever opens it, in the character's own words and opening with a cue for the mood; not the audition text, and not a generic self-introduction. The cue names a manner or an attitude (`[calm]`, `[warm, relaxed]`), not a volume or an effort (`[quietly]`, `[小声]`, `[耳语]`): in one weak reading `[quietly]` made the line breathy, quieter and flatter in pitch; the other volume and effort wordings were not tested and are treated the same. / **每个声音都交一句照角色卡写的卡片台词**：录音做的和设计出来的都一样：一到两句，念出来约 5 到 8 秒，是角色在这张卡的情境里对打开卡片的人说的话，措辞照角色的说话方式，开头写一个合这张卡情绪的提示；不念试听文本，也不写成通用的自我介绍。提示写说法或态度（`[calm]`、`[warm, relaxed]`），不写音量、力气（`[quietly]`、`[小声]`、`[耳语]`）：`[quietly]` 量过一次，读数弱：它让这一句带上气声，更轻，音高也更平；别的音量、力气的写法没测过，按同样处理。
- **One round without asking.** `references/character-voice.md` says how an agent chooses a voice, makes the pack and voices the card line without asking anyone (for example, making a character in the background), and how "another one" goes: what it decides in the user's place (a neutral gender when nothing tells, the pack name, `--short-name` left as `vp`), one design candidate checked with `make-pack --dry-run --json` on the temporary pack, and the change made, after a new temporary pack and acted take, as a new version of the same pack with every previous segment dropped. / **不问人做完一轮**：`references/character-voice.md` 写 agent 怎么不问人就选好声音、做好包、配好卡片那一句（例如在后台替使用者建一个角色），以及「换一个」怎么做：原来要问使用者的地方怎么定（看不出性别写中性、包名、`--short-name` 不给就是 `vp`），设计候选一次一个、先用临时的包的 `make-pack --dry-run --json` 看，换出来的也照走临时的包和带表演的那一段，做成同一个包的新版本，上一版的段全部去掉。
- **`SKILL.md` no longer promises a cost total.** The last step of synthesis says what the user agreed to before paying, an upper bound from the adapter's `billing`; the report does not say what was actually spent, which is in the environment's own bills. / **`SKILL.md` 不再说汇总费用**：合成的最后一步交代的是花钱之前使用者同意的、按适配器的 `billing` 算的上限；报告里没有实际花了多少，实际花费看运行环境自己的账单。

## 2.7.0 — 2026-11

A voice pack can keep pinned values in named setups, and each item of how a line is said is taken on its own; a line is one request, with a laugh or a sigh written where it is said.
音色包可以把固定的值存成几套命名的设定，一句台词「怎么说」的每一项分别取；一行台词是一个请求，笑和叹气写在它说出来的位置。

- **Setups in the pack** (protocol core 2.7.0). The manifest takes `setups`: each has a `name` and can pin the language, emotion, style, energy, speed and a note to the model (an accent, say); at most one is marked `default`. `make-pack --setups <file.json>` writes them, and a new version keeps the previous setups unless others are given. `dub --setup <name>` picks one, and with several packs `--setup <pack>=<name>` (repeatable) picks one per pack; without it each pack uses its `default`. A name the pack does not have is an error that lists the pack's setups, before anything is paid. The lowest base part a pack with setups asks for is in `protocol/FORMAT.md` 4.4; older base parts ignore `setups` without an error. The app keeps a pack's setups through import, export and new versions. / **设定写进音色包**（协议核心 2.7.0）。清单收 `setups`：每套有 `name`，可以固定语言、情绪、风格、能量、语速和一段给模型的说明（例如口音），最多一套标 `default`。`make-pack --setups <文件.json>` 写进去，发新版本时沿用上一版的设定，除非另给。`dub --setup <名字>` 选一套，几个包一起配时 `--setup <包>=<名字>`（可重复）给每个包各选一套；不选就用各自的 `default`。包里没有这个名字就报错，列出包里有哪几套，在花钱之前。带设定的包要求的基础部分最低版本见 `protocol/FORMAT.md` 4.4；老版本的基础部分读到 `setups` 不报错，只是忽略。app 导入、导出和发新版本时原样保留包里的设定。
- **Each item of how a line is said is taken on its own.** Emotion, style and energy each come from the first of: what the caller gave, the script's stage direction, the setup, the `text-generation` judgement, the default reference (the order is written in full in `lib/reference-pick.mjs`). A caller who gives only an emotion no longer hides the style written in the script, such as `（耳语）`, from the reference pick. / **「怎么说」的每一项分别取**。情绪、风格、能量各自取以下第一个有的：调用方给的、台本里的舞台提示、设定、`text-generation` 的判断、默认参考（次序只在 `lib/reference-pick.mjs` 写全）。调用方只给了情绪时，台本里写的风格（例如 `（耳语）`）不再被挑参考忽略。
- **The judgement fills only what is missing, and only where there is a choice.** A line goes to `text-generation` when an item is still missing and the reference segments left after the items already decided still differ in it. The request says which items of the line are decided and asks only for the rest, so a judged style fits a pinned emotion; the answer never replaces what the caller, the script or the setup gave. A line with a single stage direction such as `（生气）` is now judged for the other items when the pack has a choice there; a language with one reference segment in the pack is no longer judged. / **判断只补缺的项，只在有得选时才判**。一句台词还缺某一项、而且按已定的项筛过以后剩下的参考段在这一项上取值不全一样，才送去 `text-generation`。请求里写明这一句哪几项已定、只问其余的，所以判出的风格配得上固定的情绪；回答不改调用方、台本、设定已经给了的。只带一个舞台提示（例如 `（生气）`）的句子，包在其余几项上有得选时，现在也会被判；包里某种语言只有一段参考，这种语言的句子不再送去判。
- **The names sent to the model are the ones the reference was picked for.** `cues` are read from the reference pick: a line with no marks now carries the judged emotion and style too, where before only marked lines had cues. A caller value outside the vocabulary leaves that cue empty instead of falling back to the stage direction. / **交给模型的名字，就是挑参考用的那几项**。`cues` 读挑参考的结果：没标的句子现在也带上判出来的情绪、风格，以前只有标了的句子才有。调用方给了词表外的值，那一项的 cue 是空的，不再退到台本的提示。
- **A line is one request** (command line and app). Pauses, laughs and sighs inside a line no longer cut it; a line is cut only where the reference, the speed or the note to the model changes, or where the adapter says it cannot take more. Where the adapter declares `cue-in-text`, a laugh or a sigh is written where it is said, as `<cue style="laugh"/>` inside `<speak>`, and the adapter turns it into the model's tag (Cohub: `[laughing]`, `[sighing]`, from voice-cloning's `takesCueInText` in its capability table, which the app reads too); it then stays out of the request's note and `cues`, so the words after it are not said laughing. A stage direction that carries a laugh together with an emotion, such as `（生气笑）`, joins the piece before it only when that piece has the same emotion; its words are not passed on as the note, the emotion goes by its name in `cues` and the laugh as `<cue/>`. Environments that do not declare `cue-in-text` behave as before. In a dry run, a unit made of several pieces lists every piece's stage directions in `pick.directions`. / **一行台词是一个请求**（命令行和 app）。一行里的停顿、笑和叹气不再把它切开；只在参考、语速或给模型的说明变了，或者适配器说收不下的地方才切。适配器声明了 `cue-in-text` 时，笑和叹气写在它说出来的位置（`<speak>` 里的 `<cue style="laugh"/>`），由适配器译成模型的标签（Cohub：`[laughing]`、`[sighing]`，依据它对照表里 voice-cloning 的 `takesCueInText`，app 读同一个字段）；这时它不进请求的说明和 `cues`，后面的话不会被当成笑着说。和笑写在一起的情绪（例如 `（生气笑）`），只在前一段情绪相同时并进去；这条舞台提示的原文不当说明交出去，情绪按规范名字进 `cues`，笑写成 `<cue/>`。没声明 `cue-in-text` 的环境行为不变。试运行里由几段并成的一截，`pick.directions` 列出每一段的舞台提示。
- **The pause limit of one request is the adapter's, and it is a total.** `limits.maxPauseInTextMs` is the longest the written pauses of one request may add up to, declared by the adapter from the model's documentation (Cohub: 10 s, where the platform keeps only the first 10 s of pauses that add up to more); past it the line is cut there. The base part's own 2.5 s is gone: an environment that declares pauses in text without a limit is not limited. / **一个请求里停顿的上限由适配器给，算的是总长**。`limits.maxPauseInTextMs` 是一个请求里写出来的停顿加起来最多多长，由适配器照模型的文档声明（Cohub：10 秒，停顿加起来超过 10 秒，平台只留前 10 秒）；超了就在那一处切开。基础部分自己定的 2.5 秒拿掉了：声明了文字里能写停顿、却没给上限的环境，不限。
- **No cut at 200 characters.** The Cohub adapter no longer gives `maxChars`, `dub` no longer cuts sentences at 200 characters, and the app and the server no longer cap the length of read-aloud text; voice cloning has no total length limit (one request of 1227 characters was measured to come out whole). The sample text of a designed voice keeps its own documented limit. / **不再按 200 字切**。Cohub 适配器不再给 `maxChars`，`dub` 不再按 200 字切句，app 和服务端也不再限制朗读的字数；克隆没有总字数上限（量过，一次 1227 个字完整出来）。声音设计的试听文本仍有它自己有出处的上限。
- **What was measured.** Judged with Studio's method ("acted or read", one clip per question), one request per line did not read as more acted than the old cuts in any of the runs, and a controlled run on long pauses read no difference either way. What is seen directly: the laugh lands where it is written and is heard in the transcript, there are fewer requests and no seams between them, and pauses come out closer to the written length. / **量到的**。照 Studio 的判法（问「演还是念」，一次一段），一行一个请求在各组数据里都没有读出比原来的切法更像在演；只改长停顿的对照，两个方向都读不出差别。直接看到的是：笑落在写它的位置上，转写里听得出来；请求更少，接缝没了；停顿的长度更接近写的数。

## 2.6.1 — 2026-11

Masking now takes time in proportion to the length of the text and knows more shapes, and a laugh or a sigh written in front of a pause no longer follows the later parts of the line.
打码的耗时跟着文字的长度走，认得出的写法多了；写在停顿前面的笑、叹气不再跟到这一行后面的几段。

- **Masking takes time in proportion to the length of the text** (protocol core 2.6.1). Error text from the platform, the model and local programs comes back whole, so long text now reaches `reportable`; on a long run of letters and digits (audio echoed back as base64, say) the time grew with the square of the length: a 1 MB run took minutes. It now takes milliseconds, and, apart from the shapes in the next item, what is masked and what is left alone is word for word what it was. / **打码的耗时跟着文字的长度线性增长**（协议核心 2.6.1）。平台、模型和本机程序的报错原话全文报回，长文字就会走到 `reportable`；一长串字母数字（比如把音频按 base64 回显在报错里）原来耗时随长度的平方增长，1 MB 要几分钟。现在是几毫秒，除了下一条的新写法，打码去掉什么、留下什么，和以前一字不差。
- **Masking knows more shapes** (protocol core 2.6.1). `Cookie:` and `Set-Cookie:` values (to the end of the line, like `Authorization`); the user name and password in a URL (`scheme://user:password@host` comes back as `scheme://[token]@host`); a key the platform echoes in its own sentence (`Incorrect API key provided: <key>`: the name of the key may be followed by `provided`, `supplied`, `given` and the like, and `api key` with a space counts as a name); `private_key` fields and PEM blocks (a block cut off before its `-----END` is masked to the end of the text); and keys that begin with a fixed prefix and stand bare in the text (`sk-…`, `AIza…`). Only shapes are read, no service is named, and the rest of the rules are as before. / **打码认得出的写法多了**（协议核心 2.6.1）。`Cookie:`、`Set-Cookie:` 的值（和 `Authorization` 一样取到行尾）；网址里的用户名和密码（`scheme://用户:密码@主机` 报回来是 `scheme://[token]@主机`）；平台在自己的句子里回显的 key（`Incorrect API key provided: <key>`：键名后面可以跟 `provided`、`supplied`、`given` 这样的一个词，`api key` 中间有空格的也算键名）；`private_key` 字段和 PEM 块（写到一半被截断、没有 `-----END` 的块，从开头一直去掉到文字末尾）；以及带固定前缀、裸写在正文里的 key（`sk-…`、`AIza…`）。只认形状，不点任何服务的名字，其余的规则和以前一样。
- **A laugh or a sigh goes only with the part right after it.** In 2.6.0 an emotion or style written in a line was carried across `[pause …]` to the end of the line, and that carried one-shot sounds too: `太好了！*笑* 我们这次竟然成功了，[pause 0.5s]我真的太高兴了，[pause 0.5s]快过来一起庆祝吧！` asked for a laugh on all three parts. Now only the part right after `*笑*` is a laugh, in its `cues`, in the note to the model and in the style the reference is picked for; after it, the emotion and style that were in force before come back (`（耳语）你好[pause 1s]*笑*哈哈[pause 1s]再见`: "哈哈" laughs, "再见" whispers). Emotion, lasting styles such as whispering, and speed are carried to the end of the line as in 2.6.0, and the later parts, which no longer differ, can be joined into one request with `pause-in-text`. / **笑、叹气只跟紧跟在它后面的那一段。** 2.6.0 里一行中写的情绪或风格会跨过 `[pause …]` 管到行尾，一下子的声音也被带了过去：`太好了！*笑* 我们这次竟然成功了，[pause 0.5s]我真的太高兴了，[pause 0.5s]快过来一起庆祝吧！` 三段都要笑。现在只有紧跟在 `*笑*` 后面的那一段是笑：`cues`、交给模型的说明、挑参考要的风格都是；这一段以后，回到它出现以前管着的情绪和风格（`（耳语）你好[pause 1s]*笑*哈哈[pause 1s]再见`：「哈哈」是笑，「再见」是耳语）。情绪、耳语这类持续的风格和语速照 2.6.0 管到行尾；后面不再不同的几段，在有 `pause-in-text` 时能并成一次请求。

## 2.6.0 — 2026-11

Limits we had set ourselves are gone: pauses last as long as they are written, a pack takes every reference segment the material gives, a recording is no longer turned away by a quality line of ours, emotion judging sends whole sentences, and a slow network no longer cuts a read of a published file. Error text from the platform, the model and local programs comes back whole, with tokens removed, and what cannot fit (a pause too long for a WAV, a pack over the format's limits) is refused before anything is generated or paid for. An emotion or a speed written before a pause marker now keeps governing the rest of the line, as the docs said. A listener's answer that did not end properly voids that one call instead of stopping the run. The fidelity notes add acted speech.
我们自己定的上限拿掉了：停顿写多长停多长，素材切出多少段参考，包里就收多少段，音质检查不再用我们自己的线拒收录音，判情绪送整句，慢网上读已发布的文件不再被总时长掐断。平台、模型和本机程序报的错是原话全文，令牌去掉；装不下的（长到一份 WAV 放不下的停顿，超出格式上限的包）在生成和花钱之前就拒。写在停顿标记前面的情绪和语速，接着管这一行后面，和文档写的一样。听者的回答没正常结束，只作废那一次，不停下整次。还原度的读法补上了有表演的角色语音。

- **Pauses are not capped at 10 seconds.** A `[pause …]` marker, a stage direction such as （停顿 12 秒）, and pauses in `dub` and `mix` last as long as they are written, and pauses next to each other add up with no cap. The only limit is the WAV format: a pause the output file cannot hold (about 13.5 hours at 44.1 kHz, mono; the RIFF size field is 4 bytes) is refused before anything is allocated, naming where it is (`pause_out_of_range` when the script is read, `mix_too_long` when the audio is joined). / **停顿不再封顶在 10 秒。** `[pause …]` 标记、（停顿 12 秒）这类舞台提示、`dub` 和 `mix` 里的停顿，写多长停多长，挨着的几处相加也不封顶。只有 WAV 格式本身的上限：成品装不下的停顿（44.1 kHz 单声道约 13.5 小时；RIFF 的长度字段是 4 字节）在分配之前就拒，指出是哪一处（读台本时 `pause_out_of_range`，拼接时 `mix_too_long`）。
- **A line takes any number of stage directions.** The cap of 1000 per line is gone: from the 1001st on, directions used to be read aloud. Each piece's `directions` now lists only the directions in its own stretch (after the previous piece, before this one), so the returned shape changed, and `pick.directions` in `dub --dry-run --json` likewise; emotion, style, speed, pauses and the reference picked are unchanged. A sentence's note to the model is the line's latest emotion or style, the slow-down or speed-up still in force (until the next one or the end of the line, as the speed itself), and this sentence's own pauses written after that emotion or style; pauses written before earlier sentences no longer carry over. / **一行里写多少处舞台提示都认。** 一行最多认 1000 处的上限拿掉了：原来第 1001 处起的提示会被念出来。每段的 `directions` 只列它自己那一截的提示（前一段之后、这一段之前），返回值的形状变了，`dub --dry-run --json` 里的 `pick.directions` 也一样；情绪、风格、语速、停顿和挑中的参考段不变。交给模型的每句说明是：同一行里最近的一处情绪或风格，还在管着的放慢或加快（和语速本身一样，管到下一个放慢、加快或行尾），加上这一句自己前面、排在那处情绪或风格之后的停顿；写在前面几句前头的停顿不再带过来。
- **Reading a published file has no total time limit** (protocol core 2.6.0). `readPublished` used to stop every request at 30 s, which cut a 2 MB read on a slow network. It now stops only when the response headers do not arrive within 300 s (`headersTimeoutMs`) or no body bytes arrive for 300 s (`idleTimeoutMs`), the defaults of Node's own fetch (undici `headersTimeout`, `bodyTimeout`); a read that keeps moving is never stopped. `timeoutMs` still sets a total limit when a caller gives one. / **读已发布的文件不设总时长**（协议核心 2.6.0）。`readPublished` 原来每次请求 30 秒就停，慢网上读一个 2 MB 的文件会被掐断。现在只在 300 秒等不到响应头（`headersTimeoutMs`）、或连续 300 秒没有新的正文字节（`idleTimeoutMs`）时才停，照 Node 自带 fetch 的默认值（undici 的 `headersTimeout`、`bodyTimeout`）；一直在传的不停。调用方自己给 `timeoutMs` 的，照它设总时长。
- **A listener answer that did not end properly voids that call.** When `audio-text-to-text` is cut off (`stopReason` `length`), stops on an error, or returns no text, the environment marks the error `details.unusable`; tagging records that segment as voided and blind listening records that pair as voided (`blind_answer_unusable`), with no retry, and the pack or fidelity run goes on. / **听者的回答没正常结束，只作废那一次。** `audio-text-to-text` 的回答被截断（`stopReason` 是 `length`）、中途出错，或者没有文字，运行环境在错误上标 `details.unusable`；打标签把那一段记作废，盲听把那一对记作废（`blind_answer_unusable`），不重试，做包和还原度照常往下做。
- **No length limit of ours on speech instructions.** The 2000-code-point check before a call is gone; it was our own number, not the platform's (the platform has taken 1990). An instruction the platform refuses comes back with the platform's own words. / **配音指引不再由我们限长。** 调用前的 2000 码位检查拿掉了：那是我们自己定的数，不是平台的（平台收过 1990）。平台不收时，报回它自己的原话。
- **A script's output is not capped at 256 MiB.** The default is the largest string Node can hold (`buffer.constants.MAX_STRING_LENGTH`). / **脚本的输出不再封顶在 256 MiB。** 默认是 Node 能装下的最长字符串（`buffer.constants.MAX_STRING_LENGTH`）。
- **A pause that cannot fit is refused before any call, and says where.** `dub` adds up all the pauses of a script when it plans, `--dry-run` included (`[pause …]` and the ones in stage directions, in the order they are joined). If one WAV cannot hold them, it stops with `mix_too_long` before the first generation, naming the sentence and the side of the pause. A pause between the limits at reading and at joining (20 hours, say) used to pass, and was refused only when the audio was joined, after every sentence had been generated and billed. The WAV is estimated from the reference segments (the highest sample rate and the most channels among them; external segments and ones that cannot be read or recognised do not count); joining uses the first sentence's actual rate, so the two can differ only on pauses of many hours. When no reference segment gives a sample rate, the plan carries a note, `pause_length_unchecked`, that this run did not check, and joining checks as before. `pause_out_of_range` now says where: its `details` is `{ marker, line, column, limitSeconds }` (the marker as written, and its line and column in the script, counted from 1), and `digits` is gone (a caller that read it should read `marker`). A pause inside a stage direction, （停顿 9999999999 秒）, is reported the same way, at the place it is first written; `readDirections` called without the script leaves `line` and `column` null. / **装不下的停顿，在任何调用之前就拒，并说清是哪一处。** `dub` 规划时（`--dry-run` 也一样）把台本里所有的停顿加起来（`[pause …]` 和舞台提示里写的，照拼接的次序）；一份 WAV 装不下，就在第一次生成之前停下，报 `mix_too_long`，点名是哪一句、哪一边的停顿。读台本时的上限和拼接时的上限之间的停顿（比如 20 小时）以前能通过，每一句都生成、计了费，到拼接才被拒。成品的格式按参考段估：采样率取参考段里最高的，声道取最多的，外部的、读不到或认不出格式的参考段不算；拼接用的是第一句的实际采样率，两边只会在长到好几个小时的停顿上有差。参考段里一段也读不出采样率时，计划里有一条注意 `pause_length_unchecked`，说明这一次没核；拼接时照常核。`pause_out_of_range` 现在说清是哪一处：`details` 是 `{ marker, line, column, limitSeconds }`（标记原文，和它在台本里的行、列，从 1 数），`digits` 没有了（读它的调用方改读 `marker`）。舞台提示里的停顿（（停顿 9999999999 秒））也一样报，位置是它第一次写在台本里的地方；调用 `readDirections` 时不给台本，`line` 和 `column` 是 null。
- **Emotion, style and speed carry across a pause marker on the same line.** `（生气）[pause 0.5s]不行，我不去。` now picks the angry reference and tells the model angry, and `[slow]你好[pause 1s]我走了` is slow in both halves. `references/markup.md` already said a stage direction or a speed tag governs to the end of the line; the pause marker used to stop it, and the reference, the speed and the note were lost without a word. The range is now: from where it is written to the end of the line, to the next one of its kind, or to the next `[voice:…]`. A new emotion or style replaces the old one; a new speed tag or speed direction ends the earlier one, so in `[slow]a[fast]b[/fast]c` c is no longer slow. A speed tag with an end tag, and `[spell]`, are still cut by a pause marker. Brackets the vocabulary does not recognise, （画外音）, are taken out of the spoken text as before and go to the model in the sentence's note, written without the brackets (`画外音`); `pick.directions` lists them with `recognized: false`. / **情绪、风格和语速，跨过同一行里的停顿标记接着管。** `（生气）[pause 0.5s]不行，我不去。` 现在挑生气的参考，说明里也是生气；`[slow]你好[pause 1s]我走了` 两段都慢。`references/markup.md` 本来就写舞台提示、语速标签管到这一行结束，可是停顿标记会把它们截断，参考、语速和说明就悄悄丢了；现在代码照文档改。范围是：从写的地方起，到这一行结束、下一个同类的提示或标签、或者下一个 `[voice:…]` 为止。新的情绪或风格整个换掉旧的；新的语速标签或放慢、加快的提示，让前面还管着的那个结束，所以 `[slow]a[fast]b[/fast]c` 里的 c 不再慢。有结束标记的语速标签和 `[spell]`，停顿标记照旧切断。括号里词表认不出的内容（（画外音））照旧从要念的话里拿掉，并写进这一句的说明交给模型（说明里是它的字，不带括号：`画外音`），`pick.directions` 里列着，`recognized` 为 false。
- **Pauses on one line go after the previous sentence, and an unclosed speed tag ends at the line break.** Two things in what `parseScript` and `dub` return changed, so a caller that reads them should look. A `[pause …]` and a pause in a stage direction on the same line add up and both belong after the previous sentence: for `你好[pause 4s]（停顿 4 秒）我走了` the first sentence has `pauseAfterMs` 8000 and the second `pauseBeforeMs` 0, where they were 4000 and 4000; the silence between the two is the same. Between lines nothing changes: the pause after one sentence and the pause before the next stay apart. And `[slow]`, `[fast]` or `[emphasis]` without an end tag ends at the line break, as `references/markup.md` said: `[slow]a` followed by a line `b` is two spans, b at the normal speed, where it was one slow span. `parseScript` takes `lineStarts: true` to mark the span that starts a line (`line_start`); `readDirections` takes the state of the earlier piece on the same line and returns what to carry on (`carry`, `headPauseMs`). / **同一行里的停顿挂在前一句后面，没关的语速标签到行尾为止。** `parseScript` 和 `dub` 返回的两样东西变了，读它们的调用方要看一下。同一行里的 `[pause …]` 和舞台提示里的停顿相加，都算在前一句的后面：`你好[pause 4s]（停顿 4 秒）我走了` 里，第一句的 `pauseAfterMs` 是 8000，第二句的 `pauseBeforeMs` 是 0，原来是 4000 和 4000；两句之间的静音总长不变。隔一行的缝上不变：前一句的后面和后一句的前面各写各的。另外，没有结束标记的 `[slow]`、`[fast]`、`[emphasis]` 到行尾为止，和 `references/markup.md` 写的一样：`[slow]a` 后面另起一行 `b`，是两个 span，b 是正常语速，原来是一个慢的 span。`parseScript` 收 `lineStarts: true`，用 `line_start` 标出哪个 span 是一行的开头；`readDirections` 收同一行里前一段留下的状态，返回要接着带的状态（`carry`、`headPauseMs`）。
- **Emotion judging sends whole sentences, and follows the model's input limit.** A sentence goes to `text-generation` whole: the cut at 500 characters is gone. Emotion judging follows the model's input limit when the environment declares one (`limits.maxPromptTokens`): lines without a label go out in as many requests as needed, each within the limit; a request whose answer is cut off is halved and asked again; a line that is over the limit on its own is not judged and not cut, and uses the default reference. `dub --dry-run` and the cost note count the requests after batching, and `judge.lines` leaves out the lines that are too long. With several packs, the numbers `judge` uses for lines (`ignored[].index`, `tooLong.lines`, `truncated.lines`) are the line's own number in the script, from 0, the same as in the prompt (`ignored[].index` was the position among the lines of its own pack). / **判情绪送整句，按模型的输入上限分批。** 一句台词整句交给 `text-generation`：截到 500 个字的做法拿掉了。环境声明了模型一次请求的输入上限（`limits.maxPromptTokens`）时，判情绪按它分批：没带标签的句子分成够用的几次请求，每次都不超过上限；回答被截断的那一批对半分开再问；单独就超过上限的句子不判、不截，用默认参考。`dub --dry-run` 和费用说明写的是分批以后的次数，`judge.lines` 不含单独超限的句子。配了几个包时，`judge` 里提到台词用的编号（`ignored[].index`、`tooLong.lines`、`truncated.lines`）是这句台词在整份台本里自己的编号，从 0 起，和发给模型的提示里的一样（`ignored[].index` 原来是它在自己那个包的台词里的次序）。
- **A pack takes every reference segment the material gives.** The cap of 24 is gone, and `over_limit` appears only when a caller gives `maxReferences`.
